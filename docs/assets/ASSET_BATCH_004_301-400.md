## World-state rule

Architecture, prop art, lighting, crowd, damage and travel visuals are projections. Accessibility, collision, route availability, event state and discovery remain authoritative gameplay/world data.

## DONE gate

All 100 units require blueprint/manifest lineage and a documented reuse/integration target. Generic modules do not become canon locations merely by being drawn.
