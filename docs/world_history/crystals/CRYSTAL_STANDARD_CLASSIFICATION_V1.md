# CRYSTAL_STANDARD_CLASSIFICATION_V1 — Standard Crystal Classification

Status: ACTIVE_CAMPAIGN_CANON_FRAMEWORK  
Authority: USER_AUTHORIZED_2026-09-29_WITH_DRAFT_TECHNICAL_DETAIL  
Initial standardization: 2506  
Present use: historical foundation for later 2670 systems

## Design rule

A crystal is never classified by a single number alone.

Five independent descriptors are required:
1. SOURCE;
2. STABILITY;
3. OUTPUT;
4. BIOACTIVITY;
5. HAZARD.

This prevents a biologically dangerous crystal with low energy output from being treated as "weak" and prevents a high-output stable industrial crystal from being mistaken for a combat-grade biological specimen.

## 1. Source class

### SRC-GEO
Geological crystal.
Forms in rock, sediment, mineral seams, subsurface deposits, or nonliving material.

### SRC-BIO
Biological crystal.
Forms within or through a living organism.

### SRC-BREACH
Breach-associated crystal.
Recovered from a portal boundary, breach zone, or transported through a breach.

### SRC-SYN
Synthetic/refined crystal.
Human-manufactured or substantially restructured from natural feedstock.

### SRC-HYB
Hybrid.
Contains inseparable characteristics from more than one source class.

### SRC-UNK
Unknown origin.

## 2. Stability class

### S0 — Inert
No measurable active output under standard test conditions.

### S1 — Stable
Safe under ordinary certified transport/storage conditions.

### S2 — Reactive
Requires controlled temperature, shielding, orientation, or field isolation.

### S3 — Volatile
May discharge, fracture, mutate, or contaminate surroundings under foreseeable stress.

### S4 — Critical
Capable of self-amplifying failure, biological propagation, or major field event.

### S5 — Unbounded/Unknown
Behavior cannot be safely bounded with current models.

## 3. Output-density band

Output is measured independently from stability.

### O0 — Trace
Detectable but unsuitable for meaningful power extraction.

### O1 — Utility
Suitable for sensors, small devices, or low-power sustained applications.

### O2 — Industrial
Useful for vehicles, machinery, local power systems, medical equipment, or heavy tools.

### O3 — Strategic
High enough to justify military control, major-grid use, advanced research, or specialized weapons.

### O4 — Extreme
Requires national-level handling controls or equivalent institutional capability.

### O5 — Exceptional
Rare output beyond normal industrial assumptions.

### OX — Nonlinear
Cannot be represented by ordinary continuous output because behavior depends on resonance, conditions, biological state, or spatial effects.

## 4. Bioactivity class

### B0 — Biologically inert
No demonstrated direct interaction beyond ordinary chemistry/radiation.

### B1 — Biologically compatible
Can contact approved tissues/materials with manageable risk.

### B2 — Bioactive
Produces measurable biological change.

### B3 — Integrative
Can become incorporated into living tissue or stable biological systems.

### B4 — Transformative
Capable of major developmental, genetic, metabolic, or anatomical alteration.

### B5 — Propagative
Can cause self-spreading biological/crystalline effects.

### BX — Unknown

## 5. Hazard class

### H0 — Routine certified handling
### H1 — Trained worker precautions
### H2 — Controlled industrial/medical handling
### H3 — Specialized hazardous-material containment
### H4 — Strategic containment and exclusion zone
### H5 — Catastrophic-risk material
### HX — Insufficient data

Hazard class considers total danger, not only energy output.

## Biological subtype: beast crystal

A beast crystal is a SRC-BIO crystal recovered from a nonhuman crystal-adapted organism.

Important:
- "beast crystal" describes biological origin, not automatic quality;
- two beasts of similar size may produce radically different crystals;
- crystal value depends on stability, output, resonance, rarity, bioactivity, condition, and application;
- harvesting method can damage a crystal and reduce its utility.

## Market/combat labels

Different military, hunting, trade, and game-facing systems may use simplified grade names.

Those aliases must map to this scientific framework rather than replace it.

Legacy/current campaign terms such as Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, and King may remain valid where already authoritative, but their exact mapping to scientific output/hazard bands is NOT established by this file.

This protects existing gameplay state while preventing accidental mechanical invention.

## Crystal record requirements

Every persistent crystal type should eventually record:
- stable crystal ID;
- source species/location;
- source class;
- stability class;
- output band;
- bioactivity;
- hazard;
- mass;
- purity;
- resonance signature;
- known applications;
- known incompatibilities;
- legal restrictions;
- market state;
- degradation behavior;
- extraction effect on quality;
- contamination status.

## Anti-drift

Do not infer:
- XP value;
- sale price;
- weapon bonus;
- crafting result;
- ability compatibility;
- Steal interaction;

from scientific classification alone. Those require separate game mechanics.
