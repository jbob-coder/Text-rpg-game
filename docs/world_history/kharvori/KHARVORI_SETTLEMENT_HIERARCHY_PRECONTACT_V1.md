Live-game clock effect: NONE

## Purpose

Defines how Kharvori settlements scale before human contact and prevents later scenes from treating every city as physically or politically identical.

## Tier K0 — isolated site

Population:
0–80 typical.

Examples:
- observatory;
- ecological station;
- corridor beacon;
- seasonal extraction camp.

Typical footprint:
0.02–0.30 km².

## Tier K1 — permanent outpost

Population:
80–1,500.

Typical footprint:
0.2–2 km².

Services:
- power;
- medical room/clinic;
- storage;
- communications;
- limited local administration.

## Tier K2 — town

Population:
1,500–40,000.

Typical developed area:
2–35 km² depending density and terrain.

Services:
- schools;
- clinic/hospital;
- local markets;
- water/waste systems;
- local council;
- transport depot.

## Tier K3 — regional city

Population:
40,000–750,000.

Typical urbanized area:
20–250 km².

Adds:
- major hospital;
- higher education;
- regional courts;
- industrial districts;
- transit network.

## Tier K4 — major metropolitan city

Population:
750,000–8,000,000+.

Typical metro envelope:
15–50 km across.

Adds:
- multiple rail/transit lines;
- specialized hospitals;
- universities;
- regional government;
- major freight/aviation;
- dense utility networks.

Examples:
- CITY_TALAR_VEY_001
- CITY_ORU_KESH_001
- CITY_VASKAR_HETH_001
- CITY_MEYRA_SAAL_001

## Tier K5 — planetary-scale strategic hub

Not necessarily a city.

Function-based sites such as:
- major portal corridor complex;
- inter-polity scientific center;
- orbital logistics center.

Example:
PORTAL_HUB_VEYRA_SAR_KHEL_001.

## Architecture/body-fit rule

Civil spaces assume Kharvori adult dimensions.

Human visitors may find:
- seats taller;
- reach heights slightly higher;
- public spaces broader;
- some controls positioned awkwardly.

Mixed-species redesign is required after contact.

## Political rule

Settlement tier does not automatically determine sovereignty.

A K2 town may have strong local autonomy.
A K4 metropolis may be under federal/commonwealth authority.
