## Decomposers

Fungal and microbial decomposer analogues are major nutrient cyclers.

Some mineral-rich ecosystems support crystal-associated chemosynthetic microbial mats.

## Agriculture

Major pre-contact agricultural systems include:
- managed ribbon-grass grain analogues;
- root/tuber crops;
- fruiting fanleaf relatives;
- wetland protein cultivation;
- managed Moru herds;
- fermentation cultures.

## Water

Potable water requires biological treatment even when chemically clean.

Urban systems use:
source protection
-> sediment/mineral control
-> biological treatment
-> disinfection
-> monitoring.

## Disease ecology

Common classes:
- respiratory;
- enteric;
- vector-borne;
- herd-associated zoonotic;
- mineral/crystal-associated toxin syndromes.

## Protected ecological zones

Pre-contact Kharvori governments already maintain:
- watershed reserves;
- migration corridors;
- breeding refuges;
- mineral/crystal extraction exclusions;
- sacred/cultural landscapes in some polities.

## Civilization interaction

Kharvori industrialization has already caused:
- habitat fragmentation;
- river engineering;
- urban wildlife adaptation;
- extraction scars;
- conservation movements.

Their environmental history is not pristine or utopian.

## First-contact relevance

Human survey teams encountering a managed landscape may misclassify it as wild if they do not recognize:
- controlled burns;
- migration fencing;
- planted producer mosaics;
- artificial water channels;
- low-profile monitoring structures.

This becomes important to the evidence standard.
