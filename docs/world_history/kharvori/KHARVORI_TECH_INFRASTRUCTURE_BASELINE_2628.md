## Technology thesis

Kharvori technology is advanced but divergent from human technology.

Neither civilization is universally superior.

## Energy

Grid mix:
- conventional thermal/geothermal;
- wind/tidal/solar analogues;
- crystal/resonance-assisted storage and stabilization;
- regional high-density resonance plants.

Resilience:
large cities use compartmentalized grids with islanding capability.

## Resonance science

Kharvori resonance engineering developed from:
- native geological crystal belts;
- biological resonance sensitivity;
- long scientific study.

Strengths:
- stable fixed-field control;
- efficient local resonance sensing;
- durable corridor anchors.

Limitations:
- historically less flexible than human destination-signature synthesis;
- route creation favors known paired or repeatedly sampled destinations.

## Portal/corridor technology

Kharvori pre-contact corridors function most reliably as:
FIXED OR RECURRENT DESTINATION PAIRS
rather than rapid arbitrary retargeting.

Typical mature corridor aperture:
- 16–30 m clear width;
- 12–22 m clear height.

Large freight modules:
typically no more than 12–18 m long in ordinary civilian systems.

## Ground transport

Major urban arterial lane:
approximately 4.0–4.4 m per vehicle lane.

Heavy freight lane:
4.5–5.2 m.

Intercity guided rail:
vehicle width approximately 3.4–4.1 m.

Passenger platform height:
0.55–0.72 m.

## Air transport

Short/medium range atmospheric flight:
common.

Heavy aircraft require:
- long prepared runways;
- large maintenance hangars;
- weather-dependent operations.

Portal travel does not replace planetary aviation.

## Communications

Planetary:
high-capacity digital networks.

Offworld:
communications depend on active corridor relays, physical data transfer, or specialized resonance relays with lower reliability.

Closed corridor:
can create information delay even when local networks remain functional.

## Medicine

Capabilities include:
- advanced imaging;
- tissue repair;
- prosthetics;
- organ support;
- microbial diagnostics;
- resonance-assisted sensing.

They do not possess perfect regeneration or universal cures.

## Computing

Highly advanced digital computation.

Architectural tendency:
more distributed civic/industrial systems and less dependence on a single centralized network in critical infrastructure.

## Construction

Common structural materials:
- advanced ceramics;
- metal composites;
- mineral-resonance composites;
- engineered timber/biomaterials in some regions.

Typical public building dimensions reflect larger Kharvori bodies:
- doors: 1.1–1.4 m clear;
- corridors: 2–3 m;
- public floor heights: 4–6 m;
- freight doors: 4–7 m wide.

## Security/defense technology

Pre-contact Kharvori possess organized territorial and corridor-defense forces.

Capabilities include:
- conventional projectile/energy weapons;
- armored vehicles;
- aircraft;
- sensors;
- hardened corridor facilities;
- electronic/resonance countermeasures.

This is normal state capacity, not evidence of hostility toward humanity.

## Technology parity rule

Future comparisons must be domain-specific.

Examples:
- human portal addressing may be more flexible;
- Kharvori fixed-corridor stabilization may be more efficient;
- one side may lead in a medical or sensor domain while lagging in another.

No blanket "more advanced species" label.
