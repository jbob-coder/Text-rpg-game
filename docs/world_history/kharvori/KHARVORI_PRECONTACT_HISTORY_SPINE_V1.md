# KHARVORI_PRECONTACT_HISTORY_SPINE_V1

Status: DRAFT_CANON_ACTIVE_FOUNDATION
Authority: ROUNDS_401_410
Reference point: human-confirmed contact in 2628
Live-game clock effect: NONE

## Purpose

Establishes that Kharvori civilization has its own causal history independent of humans.

Dates below are approximate Earth-year-equivalent intervals before 2628 until a full Kharvori civil calendar is built.

## More than 1,800 years before contact — basin urbanization

Large permanent settlements expand around:
- managed rivers;
- wetland agriculture;
- herd routes;
- regional trade.

Political authority remains local and basin-centered.

## Approximately 1,100–1,400 years before contact — mineral/resonance craft traditions

Kharvori artisans and natural philosophers learn that some native mineral/crystal structures produce repeatable field effects.

Early uses:
- navigation;
- structural inspection;
- ritual/scientific instruments;
- limited energy storage.

These traditions are not yet portal science.

## Approximately 500–700 years before contact — continental industrialization

Mechanized transport, large-scale manufacturing, modern public health, and national/federal institutions spread unevenly.

Consequences:
- rapid urbanization;
- pollution;
- worker organization;
- habitat fragmentation;
- modern environmental law.

## Approximately 260 years before contact — planetary scientific standardization

Major polities adopt interoperable:
- measurement;
- communication;
- navigation;
- medical;
- resonance-engineering standards.

This increases inter-polity trade and scientific exchange.

## Approximately 170 years before contact — first controlled high-energy resonance fields

Field control improves enough to produce stable large-scale experimental effects.

Failures include:
- equipment loss;
- local contamination;
- field-induced structural damage.

## Approximately 132 years before contact — first anchored natural corridor

A naturally recurring offworld boundary is held stable long enough for controlled instrumentation.

This is the Kharvori equivalent of the transition humans experience centuries after Veinfall, but it arises from Veyra's own scientific history.

## Approximately 104 years before contact — first unmanned offworld return mission

A probe crosses and is recovered.

## Approximately 92 years before contact — first crewed offworld transit

Kharvori complete a controlled crewed crossing and return.

## Approximately 70–85 years before contact — permanent offworld stations

Scientific and resource stations become continuously staffed.

Nareth becomes one of several recurring research/resource destinations.

## Approximately 67 years before contact — Nareth survey station founded

Stable ID:
SITE_NARETH_KHARVORI_SURVEY_STATION_2561_EQUIV

Purpose:
geology, ecology, corridor maintenance, limited material extraction.

Humanity does not know this station exists.

## Approximately 20–40 years before contact — offworld governance debates

Kharvori polities dispute:
- concession rights;
- environmental limits;
- corridor fees;
- local authority;
- scientific access.

These debates are already active before human contact.

## 2626–2628 human calendar — unknown machinery appears on Nareth

From the Kharvori perspective, unfamiliar remote devices and portal signatures become a new scientific and security problem.

They do not initially know:
- species;
- homeworld;
- intentions;
- political organization.

## Historical rule

Kharvori civilization is not built backward from the First Interworld War.

Contact interrupts an existing history rather than beginning it.
