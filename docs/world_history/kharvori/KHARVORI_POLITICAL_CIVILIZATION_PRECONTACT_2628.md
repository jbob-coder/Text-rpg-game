## Planetary population

Estimated Kharvori population on Veyra:
approximately 4.6–4.9 billion.

Additional offworld population:
small but established; exact total varies by corridor census.

Veyra is not governed by one unified world-state.

## Major political blocs

### POLITY_TALARAN_COMMONWEALTH_001

Form:
federal coastal/common-market polity.

Core strengths:
- finance;
- maritime trade;
- portal/resonance engineering;
- universities;
- high-density cities.

Political culture:
negotiated federal authority with strong municipal institutions.

### POLITY_ORUUN_ASSEMBLY_001

Form:
federation of inland city-regions and agricultural basins.

Core strengths:
- food production;
- manufacturing;
- civic engineering;
- large internal market.

Political culture:
strong regional representation and public works tradition.

### POLITY_VASKAR_PROTECTORATES_001

Form:
loose defensive/resource federation of northern/highland territories.

Core strengths:
- mining;
- heavy industry;
- frontier logistics;
- cold-region engineering.

Political culture:
more decentralized, security-conscious, locally autonomous.

### POLITY_MEYRAN_BASIN_UNION_001

Form:
river/coastal union built around water management and trade.

Core strengths:
- agriculture;
- biotechnology;
- medicine;
- wetland engineering.

Political culture:
resource-sharing treaties and basin-level governance.

Kharvori politics already contains serious disagreements before humanity is known.

Recurring disputes:

- mining limits;
- offworld concession rights;
- portal tolls;
- environmental protection;
- municipal autonomy;
- information classification;
- defense spending.

These disputes do not disappear after human contact.

## Cultural rule
