# KHARVORI_PRECONTACT_FOUNDATION_REQUIREMENTS

Status: ACTIVE_REQUIREMENT_SET
Authority: WORLD_HISTORY_MASTER_INDEX + ROUNDS_391_400
Live-game clock effect: NONE

## Purpose

Defines what must exist before Kharvori first contact is written.

This file intentionally does not assign final Kharvori facts that belong to Rounds 401–410.

## Planetary requirements

Must define:
- WORLD_KHARVORI_* stable ID;
- planetary datum;
- gravity;
- atmosphere;
- day length;
- year length;
- axial/seasonal behavior;
- oceans/landmasses;
- elevation/depth ranges;
- climate bands.

## Biological requirements

Must define:
- adult body dimensions;
- mass;
- locomotion;
- senses;
- metabolism;
- reproduction;
- lifespan;
- environmental tolerances;
- juvenile development;
- biological variation.

Architecture must be compatible with body dimensions.

## Ecological requirements

Must define:
- energy base;
- producer analogues;
- consumers;
- predators;
- scavengers;
- decomposers;
- disease/parasites;
- domesticated or managed species if any;
- agriculture/food system;
- water dependency;
- major ecological hazards.

A technological civilization cannot be placed on a world without a supporting biosphere/economy unless a specific artificial-support explanation exists.

## Civilizational requirements

Before contact:
- settlement hierarchy;
- political geography;
- transport;
- communications;
- energy;
- medicine;
- education;
- economy;
- military/security;
- historical conflicts;
- law;
- culture;
- language.

## Knowledge rule

Humanity cannot know these facts before evidence exists.

Author TRUE history may contain them.

Human PUBLIC/CLASSIFIED knowledge must be versioned separately.

## War guardrail

Do not construct Kharvori society backward from WAR_FIRST_INTERWORLD_2645_2659.

Their pre-contact civilization must make sense on its own.
