## Species

Stable ID:
SPECIES_KHARVORI_001

Classification:
native sapient Veyran organism.

General body plan:
bilaterally symmetrical, upright biped, two primary manipulator arms, two locomotor legs.

This superficial similarity to humans does not imply common ancestry.

## Adult dimensions

Typical adult standing height:
1.82–2.24 m

Typical adult mass:
88–148 kg

Shoulder breadth:
0.46–0.66 m

Chest depth:
0.27–0.39 m

Typical relaxed reach:
0.78–0.98 m from shoulder pivot

Hand:
one opposable primary digit + three grasping digits.

Foot:
four load-bearing digits with a broad rear pad.

## Locomotion

Normal posture:
upright plantigrade-to-semi-digitigrade gait depending speed.

Comfortable walking speed:
approximately 1.3–1.7 m/s

Sustained running:
comparable to trained human endurance at equivalent conditioning.

Short-burst acceleration:
somewhat greater on average than humans because of lower Veyran gravity and robust lower-limb musculature.

## Skeleton and tissue

Skeleton:
calcium-phosphate analogue with naturally occurring trace mineral lattices.

Notable feature:
dense cranial/inner-ear mineral structures support balance and limited sensitivity to strong resonance gradients.

This is a sensory adaptation, not supernatural omniscience.

## Senses

Vision:
broad visible spectrum plus limited near-ultraviolet sensitivity.

Hearing:
strong low-frequency discrimination; useful for structural vibration and distant environmental cues.

Smell:
moderate, generally more sensitive than average human smell but less dominant than vision/hearing.

Resonance perception:
detects strong local crystal-field gradients at short range; practical range varies from meters to tens of meters depending source and noise.

## Respiration

Oxygen-breathing.

Human and Kharvori atmospheres are close enough for short-term respiratory compatibility under normal pressure.

Cross-world biological exposure remains hazardous because respiratory compatibility does not imply microbial compatibility.

## Metabolism

Omnivorous.

High protein and mineral demand during growth.

Adult resting energy use:
roughly human-comparable after correcting for larger body mass.

## Reproduction

Sexual reproduction.

Typical gestation:
approximately 10.5–11.5 Earth months.

Typical birth:
one offspring.

Twin-equivalent births:
uncommon but established.

Juvenile dependency:
long.

Physical maturity:
approximately 17–21 Earth years.

Social adulthood:
varies by polity, usually within 18–24 Earth years.

## Lifespan

Typical pre-contact developed-world lifespan:
92–118 Earth years.

Verified exceptional lifespan:
above 130 Earth years is rare.

## Environmental tolerance

Comfortable unprotected thermal band:
approximately 8–31 C for ordinary activity, with cultural/clothing adaptation beyond this.

Heat stress:
rises rapidly above approximately 38 C during exertion.

Cold:
manageable with clothing; prolonged subfreezing exposure remains dangerous.

## Disease

Kharvori pathogens are not assumed automatically compatible with humans.

First-contact medicine treats:
- direct infection;
- toxin exposure;
- immune overreaction;
- microbiome disruption

as separate risks.

## Architectural implications

Ordinary Kharvori civilian design typically assumes:
- clear door width: 1.05–1.35 m
- primary corridor width: 1.8–2.6 m
- residential ceiling: 2.8–3.4 m
- public ceiling: commonly 3.4–5.5 m
- stair tread: approximately 0.34–0.42 m
- stair riser: approximately 0.15–0.18 m
- seat height: approximately 0.48–0.58 m

Mixed-species spaces require explicit redesign rather than simple scaling.

## Variation

Regional and familial variation exists in:
- stature;
- pigmentation;
- cranial morphology;
- resonance sensitivity;
- metabolism;
- disease susceptibility.

No single phenotype represents all Kharvori.

## Guardrails

- Kharvori are not Dalki by default.
- no human ability system is inferred from Kharvori biology.
- resonance sensitivity does not imply human-style abilities.
