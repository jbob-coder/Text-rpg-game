Status: ACTIVE_CAMPAIGN_CANON_FRAMEWORK  
Authority: USER_AUTHORIZED_2026-09-29  
Purpose: Keep PUBLIC, CLASSIFIED, and TRUE history separate.

## PUBLIC timeline

Before 2473, the average person lives in a world where:
- extraordinary human abilities are not accepted as a normal biological phenomenon;
- alleged supernatural events are interpreted case by case;
- no functioning crystal economy exists;
- portals are not public infrastructure;
- "beasts" are not a recognized global ecological category;
- governments publicly deny having proof of a hidden superhuman population.

## CLASSIFIED timeline

By the late pre-Crystal period, selected states and institutions know:
- genuine natural ability users exist;
- some manifestations cluster in families;
- some anomalous events interact with matter and biology;
- rare mineral samples exhibit unexplained properties;
- spatial anomalies are increasing;
- records from unrelated countries describe comparable events.

They do NOT know:
- the ultimate source of abilities;
- the ultimate origin of crystals;
- the full structure of Veinfall;
- all anomalous lineages;
- all organizations involved;
- what lies beyond future stable portals.

## TRUE timeline

World truth includes:
- natural ability users existed for millennia;
- multiple unrelated groups alternately protected, exploited, studied, hid, or killed them;
- some ancient myths contain distorted memories of real anomalies;
- anomalous physical phenomena predate Veinfall;
- Veinfall is the global threshold that changes rarity into civilization-scale reality.

## Knowledge firewall

A TRUE event is not automatically known to:
- Jack Wilson;
- Elias Voss;
- NPCs;
- modern historians;
- governments;
- the public.

Each future history file must identify who can plausibly know its contents in 2670.
