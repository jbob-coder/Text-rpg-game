## Political models

Human frontier settlements use several models.

### MODEL_EARTH_ADMINISTERED
Earth government appoints executive authority.

Strength:
clear strategic control.

Pressure:
local residents resent remote decisions.

### MODEL_CHARTER_COUNCIL
Local elected/selected council operates under Earth charter.

Strength:
responsive to local conditions.

Pressure:
jurisdiction conflict.

### MODEL_CORPORATE_CONCESSION
Company manages infrastructure under state license.

Strength:
rapid investment.

Pressure:
labor rights, monopoly, environmental conflict.

### MODEL_MIXED_AUTHORITY
Earth state + local council + concession holders share power.

Common in large settlements.

## Revenue conflicts

Common:
- portal tolls;
- extraction royalties;
- local taxes;
- environmental fees;
- housing land rights;
- emergency-reserve funding.

## Frontier political pressure

A settlement becomes politically harder to govern remotely when it gains:
- local food production;
- local manufacturing;
- alternate portal routes;
- large civilian population;
- permanent institutions;
- locally born generation.

## Security

Security responsibilities are divided among:
- local civil authorities;
- portal security;
- Earth military;
- corporate security where legal;
- specialist creature/quarantine response.

No one actor automatically controls all domains.
