## Definition

Veinfall is the name later historians give to the synchronized global cascade in 2473 that converts rare, containable anomalous phenomena into a persistent civilization-scale reality.

Veinfall does not create the underlying phenomenon from nothing. It changes scale, stability, frequency, and accessibility.

## Why it is called Veinfall

During the first hours, orbital, atmospheric, oceanic, and ground sensors record branching bands of anomalous energy linking otherwise unrelated disturbance zones. On maps they resemble luminous vascular or mineral-vein networks appearing across the planet and then breaking apart.

Emergency analysts begin calling them "veins." When many of the mapped bands collapse into localized crystal zones, portal tears, and biological events, the term "Veinfall" spreads from technical shorthand into public language.

The name is descriptive. It is not proof that a literal planetary vein structure exists.

## Primary cascade

### PHASE_VEINFALL_01 — Synchronization

Approximate period: hours 0–6

Observed:
- simultaneous sensor anomalies on several continents and ocean basins;
- temporary electromagnetic and gravitational irregularities;
- unusual thermal gradients without conventional heat sources;
- mineral samples in sealed laboratories begin changing structure;
- latent natural ability users report headaches, sensory distortion, involuntary activation, or sudden first manifestation;
- communications failures occur near high-intensity zones.

Knowledge state:
- PUBLIC: initially treated as unrelated infrastructure failures and disasters.
- CLASSIFIED: anomaly programs recognize matching signatures.
- TRUE: these incidents are parts of one global physical event.

### PHASE_VEINFALL_02 — Crystallization

Approximate period: hours 6–30

Observed:
- anomalous mineral growth appears in rock, soil, water systems, industrial materials, and biological tissue in some exposure zones;
- many formations are unstable and decay;
- some remain persistent;
- some organisms survive internal crystallization and develop stable crystal-associated metabolic structures;
- exposure kills many organisms rather than empowering them.

Important rule:
Crystal formation is not automatically beneficial and not every crystal is usable.

### PHASE_VEINFALL_03 — Spatial Breach

Approximate period: day 2–day 5

Observed:
- short-lived spatial tears appear;
- a smaller number stabilize long enough to exchange matter, atmosphere, microorganisms, spores, water, and animals;
- some breaches connect to survivable environments;
- others expose Earth to toxic, high-pressure, low-pressure, radiological, thermal, or biologically dangerous conditions;
- many early breaches collapse before meaningful exploration.

This is the first globally verified portal phenomenon.

Humanity cannot yet create or safely control portals.

### PHASE_VEINFALL_04 — Biological Cascade

Approximate period: day 3–day 9

Observed:
- mass mortality near severe exposure zones;
- mutation and developmental disruption;
- rapid behavioral changes in some surviving animals;
- fungal, bacterial, and plant responses;
- introduction of offworld microorganisms and organisms through breaches;
- first confirmed crystal-bearing hostile fauna;
- disruption of migration and breeding cycles.

The later term "beast" emerges from crisis language, not formal taxonomy.

### PHASE_VEINFALL_05 — Human Manifestation Surge

Approximate period: day 3–day 11

Observed:
- natural ability manifestation rises sharply above historical baseline;
- previously latent carriers can manifest under severe exposure or stress;
- some non-natural effects appear after direct crystal exposure;
- medical systems cannot initially distinguish natural abilities, exposure pathology, mutation, and crystal-induced capability.

This phase permanently destroys the viability of total global secrecy regarding superhuman phenomena.

### PHASE_VEINFALL_06 — Fragmentation

Approximate period: day 7–day 11

Observed:
- the global branching signature loses coherence;
- some zones return close to baseline;
- others remain anomalously active;
- persistent crystal deposits and biological hotspots remain;
- a minority of spatial breaches become recurring sites;
- national responses diverge sharply.

The end of the primary cascade does not mean the crisis is over.

## Extended instability: 2473–2474

For roughly 18 months after the primary 11-day cascade:
- new local crystal events continue;
- unstable portals appear unpredictably;
- governments establish exclusion zones;
- black markets form around recovered material;
- laboratories race to reproduce energy effects;
- ecological invasions establish footholds in some regions;
- medical systems create first anomaly-exposure protocols;
- ability users are registered, hidden, recruited, feared, celebrated, trafficked, or weaponized depending on jurisdiction.

1. UNAFFECTED/LOW-EXPOSURE — ordinary ecology remains dominant.
2. TRANSIENT-EXPOSURE — temporary anomalies with limited persistence.

## Ability consequences

Natural ability users existed before Veinfall.

- manifestation frequency;
- public visibility;

- legal classification;

It does NOT establish that crystals are the original source of all natural human abilities.

## Early crystal classes

Working emergency classifications created during 2473:

- CRYSTAL_RAW_UNSTABLE — recently formed, unpredictable.
- CRYSTAL_BIOLOGICAL — formed or stabilized within an organism.
- CRYSTAL_GEOLOGICAL — persistent nonliving deposit.
- CRYSTAL_BREACH_ASSOCIATED — recovered at or through a spatial breach.
- CRYSTAL_INERT — structurally unusual but no measurable active output under available tests.

These are historical emergency classes, not necessarily the taxonomy used in 2670.
