## Authority and provenance states

- CONFIRMED_CANON — explicit user instruction or already-promoted compatible campaign fact.
- DERIVED_CANON — necessary consequence of confirmed facts; may be superseded by stronger evidence.
- DRAFT_CANON — assistant-authored connective material authorized by the user, pending later review.
- CLASSIFIED_TRUE — true in world but not publicly known.
- PUBLIC_BELIEF — what ordinary people/institutions generally believe; may be incomplete or false.
- UNKNOWN — deliberately unresolved.

## Foundational world rules

1. Pre-Crystal Earth was broadly recognizable as Earth, with hidden divergences.
2. Rare anomalous humans existed for millennia before Veinfall.
3. Their existence was fragmented across bloodlines, institutions, myths, sealed records, and isolated government programs.
4. Natural human abilities are biologically integrated and usually express as one underlying ability architecture with multiple techniques.
5. Ability predisposition can be inherited, but exact manifestations are probabilistic.
6. Modern classification families include PHYSICAL, SENSORY, BIOLOGICAL, ENERGY, MENTAL, SPATIAL, TRANSFORMATION, MATERIAL, FIELD, and ANOMALOUS.
7. Natural human abilities and later crystal-induced capabilities are fundamentally distinct mechanisms.
8. Veinfall made crystal phenomena, transformed ecology, portals, and anomalous biology impossible to keep globally hidden.
9. Crystals are organized matter associated with extradimensional physical phenomena; their ultimate origin remains unresolved.
10. Crystal influence can affect microbes, fungi, plants, animals, soil, water, and human biology.
11. Beast ecologies must be simulated as ecosystems, not just enemy lists.
12. Beast origins may include transformed terrestrial organisms, offworld organisms, and later adapted/hybrid lineages.
13. Portal environments range from habitable to lethally hostile.
14. Human political responses to Veinfall were uneven: quarantine, militarization, secrecy, research, exploitation, collapse, and cooperation all occurred.
15. Crystal technology developed gradually: anomaly -> research object -> strategic resource -> market -> energy/medicine/industry/military infrastructure.
16. Human conflict over crystal resources predates major interworld wars.
17. Kharvori history exists independently before human contact and must be documented from their own causal perspective.
18. World history is tracked at minimum as PUBLIC, CLASSIFIED, and TRUE layers.
19. Missing connective canon may be authored as DRAFT_CANON without interrupting play, but major pillars must remain traceable and reversible.
20. Historical documentation may cover population, climate, borders, cities, technology, disease, food, transport, education, economy, religion, crime, law, military affairs, language, culture, species, and ecosystems.
