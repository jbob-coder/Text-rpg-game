# OFFWORLD_SETTLEMENT_LIFECYCLE_2575_2605

Status: ACTIVE_REFERENCE_WITH_DRAFT_CANON_DETAIL
Live-game clock effect: NONE

## Stage 0 — Remote survey

Population:
0 permanent.

Assets:
probes, sensors, beacons.

## Stage 1 — Security/science camp

Population:
typically tens.

Needs:
hardened shelter, emergency return, environmental monitoring, medical reserve.

## Stage 2 — Permanent research outpost

Population:
dozens to low hundreds.

Adds:
continuous staffing, redundant utilities, waste handling, protected storage.

## Stage 3 — Extraction/agricultural outpost

Population:
hundreds.

Adds:
industrial equipment, cargo staging, worker housing, stronger medical and security.

## Stage 4 — Charter settlement

Population:
hundreds to thousands.

Adds:
families, school, market, local council, civil law, larger food/water systems.

## Stage 5 — Frontier town/network

Population:
thousands or more.

Adds:
local production, diversified employment, local politics, emergency reserves, secondary settlements.

## Design principle

A successful settlement gradually reduces dependency on daily portal throughput.

It does not become independent of the portal immediately.

## Social consequence

Settlement age matters.

A 20-year-old frontier town has residents born there who do not experience Earth as their only natural home.

## Failure modes

- route collapse;
- disease;
- water failure;
- agricultural failure;
- labor abandonment;
- corporate insolvency;
- security crisis;
- environmental contamination;
- evacuation.

Failed sites may remain as abandoned scenario locations.
