# TECH_CRYSTAL_INFRASTRUCTURE_REGISTRY_2506_2558

Status: DRAFT_CANON_REGISTRY  
Authority: USER_FULL_LIBERTY_2026-09-29

## TECH_CRYSTAL_CELL_GEN1_2509

First standardized sealed crystal power cell family.

Use:
- emergency systems;
- remote sensors;
- military field equipment;
- medical backup.

Limitations:
- expensive;
- specialized shielding;
- limited repairability;
- source-dependent performance.

## TECH_RESONANCE_ASSAY_2510

Standard laboratory platform used to measure:
- field response;
- output curve;
- instability;
- contamination;
- material compatibility.

Historical significance:
Enables interoperable crystal trade.

## TECH_CRYSTAL_GRID_STABILIZER_2519

Stationary system that uses stable crystal response to smooth high-load electrical grids.

It supplements rather than replaces conventional generation.

## TECH_BIOCRYSTAL_IMAGER_2522

Medical diagnostic platform capable of visualizing abnormal crystal-associated tissue activity and some anomaly-linked metabolic processes.

It becomes important for:
- exposure injury;
- implant rejection;
- beast-derived infection;
- mutation monitoring.

## TECH_BEAST_CORE_EXTRACTION_PROTOCOL_2526

Not a machine but a standardized procedural technology.

Defines:
- safe carcass approach;
- contamination isolation;
- crystal extraction windows;
- tissue preservation;
- chain of custody;
- quality-loss documentation.

This turns beast hunting from improvised salvage into regulated industry.

## TECH_CRYSTAL_COMPOSITE_ARMOR_2536

Multi-material armor combining conventional structural layers with crystal-responsive reinforcement.

Advantages:
- high protection-to-mass in selected threat profiles;
- tunable thermal/energy behavior.

Disadvantages:
- expensive;
- failure can be nonlinear;
- requires certified maintenance.

## TECH_ADAPTIVE_PROSTHETIC_INTERFACE_2542

Medical interface using controlled crystal-responsive materials to improve prosthetic feedback and adaptation.

This is one of the ethical successes later used to justify more aggressive augmentation research.

## TECH_BIOSTABILIZATION_VAT_2549

Classified developmental platform for maintaining crystal-compatible engineered tissue.

Legitimate goals:
- organ repair;
- burn treatment;
- toxic-exposure recovery;
- beast-tissue research.

Historical consequence:
Its descendants become technical precursors to later homunculus development programs.

## TECH_BREACH_ANCHOR_ARRAY_MK1_2553

Large fixed installation capable of reducing fluctuation around a naturally recurring breach.

Cannot select destination.
Cannot create a portal from nothing.
Cannot guarantee return transit.

## TECH_BREACH_ANCHOR_ARRAY_MK3_2558

End-of-era experimental array.

Capabilities:
- maintain some pre-existing breach geometries for longer periods;
- improve probe survivability;
- reduce some boundary turbulence;
- collect better destination data.

This technology is the immediate bridge into ERA_PORTAL_EXPANSION.

## Technology record rule

Every later technology must specify:
- energy source;
- required crystal classes;
- conventional components;
- maintenance burden;
- failure modes;
- licensing;
- civilian/military availability;
- lifecycle cost;
- environmental waste;
- compatibility with portal/offworld operation.
