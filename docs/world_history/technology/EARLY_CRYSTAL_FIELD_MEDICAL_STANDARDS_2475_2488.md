## Purpose

Defines the first repeatable field equipment and occupational/medical practices of the Early Crystal Era.

These are not 2670-grade technologies.

## FIELD_KIT_ECR_2480

Typical team equipment:
- sealed sample cases;
- chemical/particulate respirators;
- layered protective suit;
- handheld thermal/field-response meter;
- biological swab kit;
- tagging beacons;
- veterinary restraint gear;
- portable barrier tape/panels;
- trauma pack;
- contamination bags;
- conventional sidearm/rifle where jurisdiction permits.

Typical carried mass:
18–32 kg per specialist depending role.

Limitations:
- poor sensitivity compared with later instruments;
- limited battery endurance;
- no reliable identification of unknown portal biology;
- protective suits reduce mobility and heat tolerance.

## STORAGE_CELL_GEN0_2479

Purpose:
stable short-to-medium-term storage of selected crystal samples.

Typical chamber:
- 1.8 × 1.2 × 1.2 m internal secure bay per modular cell;
- independent ventilation or sealed inert environment by sample type;
- mechanical isolation mount;
- temperature monitoring;
- external emergency jettison/containment procedure.

Not suitable for:
- highly volatile unknown samples;
- active breach-associated events;
- propagative biological crystals.

## OCCUPATIONAL_SCREENING_PROTOCOL_2481

Required screening categories for high-risk workers:
- pulmonary;
- neurological;
- tissue imaging;
- metabolic;
- crystal particulate burden;
- manifestation/exposure history;
- psychological stress;
- reproductive health where exposure profile warrants.

## MANIFESTATION_DIAGNOSTIC_PROTOCOL_2483

Provisional categories:
- NATURAL_MANIFESTATION_PROBABLE
- EXPOSURE_INDUCED_PROBABLE
- MIXED
- UNKNOWN

Evidence used:
- pre-event history;
- family clustering;
- tissue crystal burden;
- persistence outside anomaly field;
- repeatability;
- metabolic signature;
- neurological correlation.

No single test is decisive.

## CREATURE_RESPONSE_CAPTURE_STANDARD_2484

Preferred escalation:
OBSERVE
-> ISOLATE
-> DETER
-> RESTRAIN/CAPTURE
-> LETHAL RESPONSE IF REQUIRED.

Exceptions:
Immediate lethal threat may skip intermediate steps.

Evidence preservation:
- body location;
- tissue condition;
- crystal structure;
- environmental sample;
- estimated age/sex where possible;
- injury caused by response team.

## PORTABLE_DECON_FRAME_2485

Transportable corridor:
- deployed footprint: approximately 9 × 4 m
- clean/dirty separation;
- equipment rinse;
- particulate collection;
- disposable garment isolation.

Use:
field teams, temporary checkpoints, small labs.

## Occupational rule

A Crystal Era profession is not defined only by extraordinary hazards.

Ordinary fatigue, heat, falls, vehicle crashes, bad lifting, sleep loss, and stress remain major injury sources.

The reference system must preserve both ordinary and anomalous risk.
