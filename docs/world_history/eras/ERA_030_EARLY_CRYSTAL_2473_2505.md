## Era thesis

The first Crystal Era generation does not inherit a mature crystal civilization. It builds one while dealing with ecological disruption, unreliable portals, dangerous materials, political panic, hidden pre-Veinfall archives, emerging public ability users, and intense competition for knowledge.

The defining historical movement is:

CRISIS -> CONTAINMENT -> CLASSIFICATION -> REPRODUCIBLE SCIENCE -> REGULATION -> EARLY INDUSTRY

## Phase A — Emergency Survival, 2473–2477

Primary priorities:
- evacuation and exclusion zones;
- water and food safety;
- treatment of crystal exposure;
- quarantine of unknown organisms;
- containment of spatial breaches;
- identification of stable versus unstable crystal samples;
- management of suddenly visible ability users;
- restoration of communications and logistics.

Political patterns:
- emergency law expands;
- military and civil-defense organizations gain power;
- some jurisdictions criminalize unregistered crystal possession;
- others subsidize civilian collection and research;
- black markets appear immediately;
- pre-Veinfall secret anomaly programs are partially exposed, reorganized, or destroyed.

## Phase B — Classification and Replication, 2478–2488

Scientific priorities:
- distinguish crystal chemistry from ordinary minerals;
- determine energy output conditions;
- map exposure effects;
- identify crystal-bearing biological tissues;
- separate natural human abilities from crystal-induced effects;
- develop safe storage;
- reproduce small controlled outputs;
- establish portal observation standards.

Key conceptual breakthrough:
A crystal is not treated as "magic fuel." Different samples have different structure, stability, biological origin, field response, and failure behavior.

Early standards begin assigning:
- origin;
- purity;
- stability;
- energy-response profile;
- biological compatibility;
- contamination risk;
- breach association;
- legal handling class.

## Phase C — Early Regulated Use, 2489–2505

By the end of the era, limited crystal technology enters controlled civilian and industrial use.

Early applications:
- emergency power modules;
- specialized medical imaging/sensing;
- hazardous-environment detectors;
- secure research batteries;
- military field systems;
- portal-site stabilization equipment;
- industrial heat/energy processes;
- biological containment tools.

Still NOT mature:
- cheap universal crystal power;
- safe personal crystal enhancement;
- routine artificial portals;
- unrestricted offworld travel;
- fully mapped beast ecologies;
- reliable cure for severe crystal mutation.

## Ability-user society

Veinfall forces legal systems to answer questions previously avoided:

- Is an ability use equivalent to a weapon?
- Can a person be compelled to register?
- Can employers discriminate by ability risk?
- Can militaries conscript or recruit ability users differently?
- Who owns biological crystal material removed from a person?
- Are crystal-induced abilities legally equivalent to natural abilities?
- Who is liable for involuntary manifestation damage?

Different jurisdictions answer differently.

This produces the first durable ability-rights, ability-control, and anti-ability political movements.

## Hidden pre-Crystal archives

The Crystal Era exposes fragments of earlier history.

Consequences:
- old lineage records become strategically valuable;
- governments discover rival states knew more than publicly admitted;
- some families are targeted for genetic/medical study;
- false lineage claims become profitable;
- surviving ORG_LEDGER_CIRCLE_001 records gain new importance;
- ORG_MERIDIAN_CONCORDAT_001 and ORG_VEILWATCH_DIRECTORATE_001 archives become foundations for new institutions.

No archive contains the complete truth.

## Ecology

Early Crystal Era ecology is unstable rather than standardized.

Regional outcomes:
- some breach species fail quickly;
- some become invasive;
- some native species die;
- some native species adapt;
- crystal-bearing reproductive lineages emerge;
- new pathogens cross ecological boundaries;
- human eradication campaigns alter food webs;
- protected research zones become unintended refugia.

The first serious "beast ecology" discipline develops because killing every dangerous organism proves ecologically and economically unsustainable.

## Economy

2473–2477:
- crystals are primarily hazardous strategic material.

2478–2488:
- research value exceeds bulk energy value for many samples.

2489–2505:
- standardized samples become tradable commodities;
- licensing, insurance, transport law, secure storage, and state reserves appear;
- criminal extraction and smuggling remain major problems.

## Military development

Early military use focuses on:
- detection;
- containment;
- protective materials;
- powered support systems;
- specialized ammunition/field tools;
- ability-user doctrine;
- anti-beast tactics;
- portal perimeter defense.

Crystal weapons are initially expensive, unreliable, and maintenance-heavy.

## Portal science

Natural breaches are studied throughout the era.

Humanity can:
- detect many active breaches;
- measure stability;
- create perimeter containment;
- sometimes prolong a naturally occurring opening;
- transmit probes through suitable breaches.

Humanity cannot yet:
- freely choose destinations;
- mass-produce safe portal generators;
- guarantee return routes;
- treat portal travel as ordinary transport.

## Information culture

Public knowledge expands rapidly but remains distorted by:
- government secrecy;
- trauma;
- misinformation;
- sensational media;
- commercial claims;
- religious interpretation;
- forged crystals;
- fake ability cures;
- false beast sightings.

This era is where PUBLIC, CLASSIFIED, and TRUE timelines diverge most sharply.

## End state by 2505

Humanity has survived Veinfall and proven that crystal phenomena can be measured, classified, regulated, and technologically exploited.

The world is no longer merely reacting.

It is beginning to build a civilization around the phenomenon.
