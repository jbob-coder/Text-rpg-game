## Era thesis

Human civilization develops without public knowledge of crystals as a world-transforming resource. Rare humans nevertheless manifest real abilities for millennia. Their scarcity, inconsistent inheritance, geographic isolation, limited recordkeeping, deliberate concealment, and frequent reinterpretation as religion or folklore prevent society from recognizing a unified phenomenon.

The pre-Crystal world is therefore neither fully mundane nor openly superhuman.

## Natural anomalous humans

Stable ID: HUMAN_LINEAGE_ANOMALOUS_BASELINE_001

Modern technical interpretation:
- anomalous humans remain Homo sapiens;
- manifestation is tied to a rare heritable coupling phenotype;
- inheritance changes probability and compatibility rather than copying an exact power;
- most manifest individuals express one underlying ability architecture;
- one architecture may support many techniques;
- multiple genuinely independent natural ability architectures in one person are extraordinarily rare;
- manifestation severity, age of onset, control, and detectability vary.

Estimated pre-Veinfall manifest frequency:
- broad historical average: roughly 1 per 5–20 million humans;
- local frequencies may be much higher in some lineages;
- carriers without obvious manifestation are more common than active users.

The exact molecular mechanism remains incomplete even in 2670.

## Public, classified, and true history

### TIMELINE_PUBLIC_PRECRYSTAL

Ordinary public understanding before 2473:
- supernatural claims are generally folklore, fraud, religion, legend, misdiagnosis, or unexplained events;
- governments deny possessing evidence of a distinct anomalous human population;
- isolated viral incidents periodically generate public controversy but no globally accepted proof.

### TIMELINE_CLASSIFIED_PRECRYSTAL

Known to restricted institutions by the late pre-Crystal period:
- rare human abilities are real;
- some traits show familial clustering;
- abilities do not fit one simple mechanism;
- anomalous materials and spatial disturbances are increasing;
- some historical archives contain credible earlier cases.

### TIMELINE_TRUE_PRECRYSTAL

Authoritative world truth:
- natural ability users have existed for millennia;
- no single secret organization controls them all;
- bloodlines, states, private researchers, religious institutions, criminals, protectors, and exploiters each possess fragments;
- the crystal-associated phenomenon is physically real before Veinfall but has not yet become a stable global ecological-industrial system;
- Veinfall is a threshold event, not creation ex nihilo.

## Social consequences before Veinfall

Because abilities are rare, an individual user's fate depends heavily on time and place. Documented patterns include:

- deification or ritual status;
- fear and ostracism;
- medicalization;
- military recruitment;
- family concealment;
- criminal exploitation;
- protective patronage;
- scientific study;
- execution or disappearance;
- deliberate anonymity.

No universal historical treatment applies.

## Technology and ecology

Before Veinfall:
- mainstream technology follows non-crystal development;
- no civilian crystal grid exists;
- no mature crystal economy exists;
- portals are not reliable public infrastructure;
- terrestrial ecosystems remain overwhelmingly ordinary;
- stable beast ecologies have not yet displaced normal regional food webs;
- anomalous incidents are local enough to be contained, misidentified, or lost.

## End of era

By 2472, the number and geographic spread of anomalies exceeds the ability of existing secrecy systems to model or contain them.
