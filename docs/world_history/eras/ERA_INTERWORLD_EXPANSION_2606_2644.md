## Era thesis

Portal Expansion proves that humans can live beyond Earth.

Interworld Expansion asks:
Who governs, supplies, protects, taxes, represents, and claims the worlds connected by those portals?

The early causal chain is:
SETTLEMENT GROWTH
-> ROUTE DIVERSIFICATION
-> LOCAL ECONOMIES
-> FRONTIER POLITICS
-> MULTI-WORLD STRATEGY.

Kharvori first contact and later escalation belong inside this era, but their causal chain must be constructed from an independent Kharvori history rather than retrofitted around the war.

## Phase I — Human consolidation, 2606–2620

Built in Rounds 391–400.

Characteristics:
- existing settlements become towns;
- additional P3/P4 routes open;
- portal hubs gain civilian customs, freight, housing, schools, courts, clinics, and reserve systems;
- offworld jurisdictions become economically differentiated;
- Earth governments compete for access without open interworld war.

Representative networks:
- HAVEN_03 — mature mixed civilian/resource network;
- CINDER_05 — extraction-heavy frontier;
- VERDANT_11 — biosecure agricultural/research frontier.

These are human operational designations, not indigenous names.

Kharvori civilization:
- predates human discovery;
- has its own ecology, politics, technology, disputes, and historical memory;
- is not created by portal contact;
- is not a Homunculus creation;
- must not be reduced to a war enemy species.
