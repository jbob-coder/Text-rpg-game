# ERA_000_PREHUMAN_FOUNDATIONS — Prehuman Foundations

Status: ACTIVE_CAMPAIGN_CANON  
Authority: USER_AUTHORIZED_2026-09-29_WITH_DRAFT_CONNECTIVE_DETAIL  
Scope: Formation of Earth through the emergence of Homo sapiens  
Live-game clock effect: NONE

## Purpose

This era establishes the physical and ecological baseline that later Crystal Era changes must be measured against. The world begins as a recognizable Earth governed by ordinary astrophysical, geological, chemical, and biological processes. Crystal phenomena are not the default explanation for normal evolution.

## Core canon

- Earth forms approximately 4.54 billion years before the modern era.
- Continents, oceans, atmosphere, plate tectonics, mass extinctions, and biological evolution proceed broadly comparably to real Earth.
- Complex multicellular life, terrestrial ecosystems, mammals, primates, and hominins arise through ordinary evolutionary processes.
- There is no confirmed planet-wide crystal biosphere in deep prehistory.
- No confirmed Kharvori settlement, offworld colony, or large portal civilization exists on prehistoric Earth in current canon.
- The ultimate extradimensional phenomenon later associated with crystals may predate humanity, but whether it ever interacted measurably with primordial Earth remains UNKNOWN.

## Ecological baseline

Stable ID: ECOSYSTEM_PRECRYSTAL_EARTH_BASELINE_001

Before sustained anomalous influence, Earth ecosystems operate through recognizable terrestrial mechanisms:

- solar energy and chemosynthesis;
- food webs and nutrient cycles;
- genetic mutation and selection;
- predation, competition, parasitism, symbiosis, disease, migration, and extinction;
- no universal "beast" category;
- no crystal-core organ as a normal biological structure;
- no stable crystal-energy trophic layer.

This baseline is important because later Crystal Era species must identify what changed rather than treating every unusual organism as if it had always existed.

## Deep-history anomaly policy

Stable ID: SECRET_DEEP_TIME_CRYSTAL_ORIGIN_001

TRUE layer:
- The fundamental origin of the crystal-associated extradimensional phenomenon is unresolved.
- Later researchers find ambiguous deep-time signatures that could indicate rare prehuman interactions, contamination from later geological processes, or measurement error.
- No current authority may convert those ambiguous signatures into a confirmed prehistoric crystal civilization without a later canon decision.

PUBLIC layer:
- Ordinary education in 2670 teaches that Veinfall marks the beginning of the Crystal Era, not the creation of the universe or the origin of life.

CLASSIFIED layer:
- Restricted scientific archives preserve a small number of anomalous mineral and isotopic samples whose dates appear older than human civilization.
- Their interpretation remains contested.

## Human evolutionary bridge

Stable ID: EVENT_HOMO_SAPIENS_EMERGENCE_001

Homo sapiens emerges as a normal terrestrial species. The rare biological architecture later associated with natural abilities is not treated as a separate species. It appears within Homo sapiens as a very uncommon heritable potential.

No prehuman hominin is currently confirmed to have expressed a stable supernatural ability. That question remains open for later paleogenetic reconstruction.

## Anti-drift rules

1. Do not retroactively make all evolution crystal-driven.
2. Do not make dinosaurs or prehistoric megafauna automatically "beasts."
3. Do not place modern ability classifications in ancient public knowledge.
4. Do not reveal the ultimate crystal origin merely because the authoring layer knows it is extradimensional.
5. Any future prehistoric anomaly must state whether it is CONFIRMED_CANON, DERIVED_CANON, DRAFT_CANON, or UNKNOWN.

## Exit condition

This era hands off to ERA_010_HUMAN_PRECRYSTAL when anatomically modern humans begin leaving recoverable evidence of rare anomalous manifestations and inherited predisposition.
