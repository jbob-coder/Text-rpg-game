# LAW_CRYSTAL_ABILITY_FRAMEWORK_2506_2558

Status: DRAFT_CANON_FRAMEWORK  
Authority: USER_FULL_LIBERTY_2026-09-29

## Core principle

No single global law governs crystals or abilities during industrialization. International standards emerge, but enforcement remains jurisdictional.

## LAW_CRYSTAL_CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY_2507

Creates standardized ownership and transfer records for regulated crystals.

Purpose:
- reduce theft;
- identify contaminated material;
- support taxation;
- support accident investigation;
- distinguish lawful harvesting from poaching.

## LAW_BIOCRYSTAL_HARVEST_LICENSE_2527

Representative model adopted by many jurisdictions.

Requires licenses for commercial harvesting of regulated crystal-bearing fauna.

Typically regulates:
- species;
- season;
- quota;
- extraction method;
- protected habitat;
- transport;
- carcass disposal.

## LAW_ABILITY_PUBLIC_SAFETY_MODEL_2514

Model framework distinguishing:
- mere possession of an innate ability;
- negligent use;
- licensed professional use;
- intentional harmful use;
- high-risk ability restrictions.

Important:
Having an ability is not automatically a crime under the model framework, though some jurisdictions are more coercive.

## LAW_CRYSTAL_AUGMENTATION_DISCLOSURE_2545

Requires specified medical or occupational disclosure for high-risk crystal integration.

Contested because:
- privacy advocates treat it as medical discrimination;
- employers argue undisclosed augmentation creates safety risk;
- militaries seek broader access to records.

## LAW_PORTAL_SITE_SOVEREIGNTY_MODEL_2556

Early attempt to define control over recurring natural portal sites.

Disputes include:
- whether a portal is part of the land beneath it;
- whether destination territory creates claims;
- whether states may block international scientific access;
- who owns resources brought back;
- liability for organisms or contamination crossing the boundary.

These unresolved disputes become major drivers of ERA_PORTAL_EXPANSION.

## Ability rights conflict

By 2558, major political arguments include:
- mandatory registration;
- hereditary lineage databases;
- military recruitment;
- ability-based employment restrictions;
- insurance;
- school accommodation;
- self-defense;
- involuntary manifestation;
- criminal sentencing;
- privacy of biological compatibility data.

There is no universal resolution.

## Game simulation requirement

Every jurisdiction should eventually specify:
- crystal possession rules;
- weapon rules;
- ability-use law;
- augmentation law;
- beast-harvest law;
- portal law;
- search/seizure authority;
- emergency powers;
- penalties;
- corruption/enforcement quality.

NPC behavior must follow local law and personal knowledge rather than one global ruleset.
