## Waste classes

Representative categories:
- inert certified residue;
- reactive mineral residue;
- contaminated packaging;
- biological remains;
- damaged crystal-cell assemblies;
- assay chemicals/solvents;
- rejected feedstock;
- unknown mixed waste.

## Disposal hierarchy

Preferred:
REUSE / RECOVER
-> STABILIZE
-> SEALED STORAGE
-> CONTROLLED DISPOSAL.

Open dumping of regulated active material is prohibited in stronger jurisdictions but still occurs through corruption or illegal contractors.

## SITE DESIGN PRINCIPLES

A compliant disposal site increasingly requires:
- groundwater separation;
- runoff control;
- lined containment;
- monitoring wells;
- fire/emergency access;
- isolation cells;
- recordable lot placement;
- buffer from housing.

## Early contamination scandals

Recurring causes:
- falsified inert classification;
- leaking containers;
- stormwater overflow;
- illegal biological disposal;
- abandoned contractor lots.

## Historical consequence

Environmental law begins treating crystal waste as a long-duration liability rather than ordinary industrial trash.
