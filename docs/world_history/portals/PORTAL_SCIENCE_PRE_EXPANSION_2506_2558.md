# PORTAL_SCIENCE_PRE_EXPANSION_2506_2558

Status: ACTIVE_CAMPAIGN_CANON_WITH_DRAFT_TECHNICAL_DETAIL  
Authority: USER_AUTHORIZED_2026-09-29

## Starting model

A natural portal/breach is a localized spatial connection between physically separated regions that can permit transfer of matter and energy.

The mechanism is associated with the same broad anomalous physical domain implicated in Veinfall and crystals, but scientific correlation is not equivalent to complete causal understanding.

## Variables measured by portal science

- aperture size;
- boundary stability;
- duration;
- destination atmosphere;
- pressure differential;
- thermal differential;
- radiation;
- field turbulence;
- matter-transfer success;
- biological contamination;
- destination motion relative to aperture;
- return-path persistence;
- resonance signature.

## 2506 capability

Humanity can detect and observe many breaches but has poor control.

Typical approach:
- perimeter;
- drones/probes;
- remote sampling;
- sacrificial sensors;
- short exposure windows;
- military containment.

## 2520s capability

Improvements allow:
- better prediction of recurring breach windows;
- remote atmosphere testing;
- stronger containment;
- limited unmanned transfer;
- mapping of repeated destinations where signatures recur.

Fatal exploration accidents remain common enough to constrain public travel.

## 2540s capability

Crystal-field engineering allows:
- reduction of edge turbulence;
- longer sensor windows;
- improved probe communication;
- limited stabilization of naturally recurring sites.

Research competition intensifies because recurring destinations may contain:
- water;
- minerals;
- biomass;
- habitable terrain;
- dangerous organisms;
- unknown technology;
- no usable resources at all.

## 2553 breakthrough

TECH_BREACH_ANCHOR_ARRAY_MK1 demonstrates that an artificial field can measurably prolong a naturally recurring aperture.

This is not yet portal creation.

## 2558 threshold

TECH_BREACH_ANCHOR_ARRAY_MK3 produces repeatable long-duration stabilization under narrow conditions.

The next scientific problem becomes:
Can humans reproduce the boundary conditions without waiting for a natural breach?

That question defines ERA_PORTAL_EXPANSION.

## Safety rule

No portal destination is presumed safe because:
- pressure may differ;
- pathogens may cross;
- local predators may detect the opening;
- atmosphere can change;
- the return path can fail;
- destination politics may exist;
- time synchronization assumptions may be wrong until measured.

## Canon guardrail

Do not assume portal science has discovered the Kharvori before the later first-contact record is explicitly written.
