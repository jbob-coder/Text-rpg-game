## Portal quarantine layers

A mature frontier hub separates:
1. destination-side dirty approach;
2. aperture chamber;
3. transfer lock;
4. initial screening;
5. medical/biological observation;
6. customs/security;
7. clean release.

Cargo may follow a separate chain.

## 2583 crisis lessons

The network must plan for organisms that:
- are microscopic;
- have delayed symptoms;
- survive on cargo;
- do not trigger ordinary pathogen tests;
- affect only some species;
- change behavior under Earth conditions.

## Emergency closure

Closure decision can be triggered by:
- biological alarm;
- destination mismatch;
- pressure/atmosphere anomaly;
- field instability;
- hostile incursion;
- sabotage suspicion;
- receiving-hub failure.

## Stranded-population design target

By 2588 certified frontier sites should maintain minimum local survival capacity measured in days/weeks, not hours.

Required categories:
- potable water;
- food;
- medicine;
- power;
- shelter;
- sanitation;
- communications;
- repair parts.

Exact reserve length varies by route criticality.

## Rescue hierarchy

LOCAL SAFE MODE
-> PRIMARY RETURN ROUTE
-> ALTERNATE HUB
-> FRONTIER RESCUE COMPACT ASSET
-> LONG-DURATION ISOLATION.

## Medical triage

Separate:
- trauma;
- exposure;
- unknown biological;
- known contagious;
- ability-related instability;
- psychological crisis.

## Security principle

Quarantine authority and tactical security authority are distinct but coordinated.

A frightened infected traveler is not automatically a hostile actor.

A hostile actor can still exploit quarantine confusion.
