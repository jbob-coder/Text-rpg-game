# ECOSYSTEM_PRECRYSTAL_EARTH_BASELINE_001 — Pre-Crystal Earth Ecology

Status: ACTIVE_CAMPAIGN_CANON  
Authority: USER_AUTHORIZED_2026-09-29  
Temporal scope: Before EVENT_VEINFALL_2473

## Baseline principle

The pre-Crystal Earth is biologically recognizable. Crystal ecology is a later disruption and integration layer, not the hidden explanation for every organism that ever lived.

## System model

Every future ecological comparison should track at least:

- primary producers;
- herbivores/primary consumers;
- mesopredators;
- apex predators;
- scavengers;
- decomposers;
- parasites/pathogens;
- pollinators and seed dispersers;
- aquatic food webs;
- soil microbiomes;
- migration corridors;
- reproduction cycles;
- seasonal pressures;
- invasive-species pressure;
- human land use.

## Pre-Veinfall anomaly load

Background anomalous exposure: EXTREMELY_LOW and spatially discontinuous.

Consequences:
- most organisms never encounter a meaningful anomalous field;
- no globally stable beast food web exists;
- unusual mutations usually die out, remain local, or are misclassified;
- rare anomalous organisms may exist but require individual records;
- there is no standardized beast-crystal harvesting industry.

## Transition variables for later eras

When Veinfall begins, each ecosystem must track:

1. anomaly exposure intensity;
2. crystal deposition;
3. portal proximity;
4. mutation rate;
5. mortality;
6. fertility changes;
7. trophic disruption;
8. migration displacement;
9. emergence of crystal-bearing organisms;
10. offworld species introduction;
11. disease exchange;
12. human containment pressure;
13. hunting/harvesting pressure;
14. habitat fragmentation;
15. long-term stabilization.

## Extinction policy

Crystal Era ecological history may include:
- local extirpation;
- global extinction;
- survival in refugia;
- domestication;
- hybrid ecological niches;
- ordinary species surviving largely unchanged;
- transformed descendant lineages.

No species becomes extinct merely for dramatic effect. Each major loss should have a causal chain.

## Gameplay relevance

This baseline enables the game to answer:
- why a species lives in a region;
- what it eats;
- what eats it;
- how it reproduces;
- whether it is native, transformed, or offworld;
- what happens if players or factions overharvest it;
- how weather, disease, portals, and crystal contamination alter encounters.
