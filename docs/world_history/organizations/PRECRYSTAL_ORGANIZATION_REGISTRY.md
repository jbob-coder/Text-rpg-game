# Pre-Crystal Organization Registry

Authority: ASSISTANT_AUTHORED_UNDER_USER_FULL_LIBERTY_2026-09-29  
Rule: No organization in this registry controls all anomalous history.

## ORG_LEDGER_CIRCLE_001 — Ledger Circle

Approximate origin: 4th century BCE  
Type: decentralized hereditary record/protection network  
Primary behavior: preserve testimony, move endangered anomalous people, maintain coded family records.

TRUE:
- Never globally unified.
- Local cells often disagree.
- Some branches protected ability users; others became coercive or elitist.
- Many records were destroyed, forged, or absorbed by later institutions.

PUBLIC:
- effectively unknown; surviving references are treated as folklore or antiquarian societies.

Use:
- ancient lineage evidence;
- hidden archives;
- conflicting genealogies;
- old safe routes and forgotten debts.

## ORG_ANOMALOUS_PHYSIOLOGY_UNIT_001 — Anomalous Physiology Unit

Established: 1896  
Type: state-linked medical/scientific program  
Origin event: EVENT_EARLY_SCIENTIFIC_ANOMALY_PROOF_1896

Purpose:
- document repeatable physiological anomalies;
- distinguish fraud from genuine cases;
- prevent uncontrolled military exploitation by rival institutions.

Failure:
- secrecy isolates the program;
- samples and records are repeatedly transferred, redacted, and partially lost.

## ORG_MERIDIAN_CONCORDAT_001 — Meridian Concordat

Established: 2228  
Type: covert multinational data-exchange framework  
Origin event: EVENT_MERIDIAN_INFORMATION_EXCHANGE_2228

Purpose:
- compare anomalous incidents across borders;
- standardize minimum terminology;
- identify transnational threats;
- prevent accidental interstate escalation caused by misidentified ability incidents.

Limitations:
- members withhold strategic cases;
- intelligence services manipulate submissions;
- family-lineage data becomes politically sensitive;
- no public mandate.

## ORG_VEILWATCH_DIRECTORATE_001 — Veilwatch Directorate

Established: 2441  
Type: late pre-Veinfall crisis-analysis coalition  
Status by 2473: active but overwhelmed

Purpose:
- track spatial anomalies, anomalous minerals, biological changes, unexplained energy signatures, and ability manifestation spikes;
- correlate environmental signals previously held in separate scientific and security databases.

Historical importance:
- correctly concludes before Veinfall that incidents are converging toward a common physical process;
- fails to predict scale, timing, or global cascade structure;
- its emergency models later become part of first-generation Crystal Era response doctrine.

## Design rule

Future organizations must specify:
- jurisdiction;
- funding;
- membership;
- real capability;
- knowledge limits;
- internal factions;
- failures;
- public cover;
- archives;
- relationship to later Crystal Era institutions.
