## Political conflict

The major institutional disputes of 2475–2488 include:
- how long emergency powers can persist;
- whether ability data is medical or security information;
- who owns crystals recovered from private land;
- whether displaced residents retain title inside permanent exclusion zones;
- who pays for ecological damage;
- whether creature-response authority belongs to police, military, wildlife agencies, or new specialist bodies.

No universal solution emerges.
