## Locked constraints

- Homunculus is currently an organizational/factional label.
- The network is clandestine and strategically hostile to humanity.
- It does not cause the First Interworld War.
- It does not explain Veinfall.
- It does not explain all beasts.
- It does not automatically explain protagonist mysteries.

## Ontology questions

Do not answer automatically:
- Are members one species?
- Are they multiple beast-derived lineages?
- Are they engineered?
- Are some ordinary humans full members?
- Is Homunculus a self-name or human label?
- Can membership be inherited?
- Is there a biological marker?
- Can a member defect?

## Threat-design rule

The network must be dangerous because of organization, capability, secrecy, and objectives—not because it possesses unlimited power.

Every major capability must have:
- cost;
- logistics;
- failure mode;
- detection risk;
- countermeasure;
- scale limit.

## Collaboration law design

Later legal records must separate:
- innocent encounter;
- coerced contact;
- reporting/intelligence contact;
- commercial contact;
- concealment;
- material aid;
- recruitment;
- sabotage;
- strategic collaboration.

Punishment is jurisdiction- and era-specific.

## Protagonist firewall

No Jack/Elias family member, ability, parent, or mystery is connected to the network without separate explicit authority.
