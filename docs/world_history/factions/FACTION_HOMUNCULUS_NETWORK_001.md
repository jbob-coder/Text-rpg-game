## Confirmed core

The Homunculus Network is a hidden society operating against the survival and long-term security of humanity.

It is not the cause of the First Interworld War.

It is not the universal explanation for crystal phenomena, portals, beast ecology, Kharvori conflict, or protagonist mysteries.

Confirmed:

## Collaboration doctrine

Human authorities may distinguish:
- accidental contact;
- coerced contact;
- intelligence-controlled contact;
- negligent trade;
- concealment;
- material support;
- recruitment;
- sabotage on behalf of the network;
- strategic collaboration.

Active knowing collaboration can be prosecuted as a crime against humanity, treason-equivalent offense, or both, depending on jurisdiction and period.

Possible penalties include capital punishment where local law permits it.

No player or NPC is presumed guilty merely because of unexplained contact.
