## Narrative history

Write coherent history, not bullet chronology.

Required qualities:
- causal continuity;
- lived experience;
- institutions acting for reasons;
- uncertainty where historically appropriate;
- consequences that carry forward.

## Representative lived experience

- worker;
- student;
- medic/scientist;

These are representative historical lenses, not player characters.
