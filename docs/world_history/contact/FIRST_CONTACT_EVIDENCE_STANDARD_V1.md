# FIRST_CONTACT_EVIDENCE_STANDARD_V1

Status: ACTIVE_REFERENCE_STANDARD
Authority: ROUNDS_391_400
Live-game clock effect: NONE

## Purpose

Prevents human survey teams from declaring intelligent nonhuman civilization from ambiguous anomalies.

## Evidence levels

### FC-E0 — AMBIGUOUS
Unusual pattern with plausible natural explanation.

Examples:
- geometric mineral formation;
- repeating biological signal;
- straight terrain feature.

Action:
observe, do not classify as civilization.

### FC-E1 — PROBABLE ARTIFICIAL
Multiple features are difficult to explain naturally.

Examples:
- repeated machining-like surfaces;
- nonrandom signal timing;
- structured debris.

Action:
halt destructive exploitation locally; escalate review.

### FC-E2 — TECHNOLOGICAL SYSTEM
Evidence supports an operating or historical technological process.

Examples:
- powered installation;
- encoded signal;
- manufactured material chain;
- controlled transportation geometry.

Action:
restrict access; dedicated intelligence/science review.

### FC-E3 — INTELLIGENT AGENT CONFIRMED
Direct evidence of responsive intelligent agency.

Examples:
- adaptive reciprocal signaling;
- observed tool-using sapient behavior;
- controlled response to human communication.

Action:
first-contact protocol; no ordinary resource claim.

### FC-E4 — CIVILIZATION CONTACT
Verified organized society with institutions, territory, language/communication system, or equivalent.

Action:
diplomatic framework supersedes ordinary frontier exploitation law.

## Confirmation rule

A single sensor result cannot establish FC-E4.

Use:
- independent sensors;
- repeated observation;
- provenance;
- contamination controls;
- interpretation review.

## Knowledge layers

TRUE:
what the phenomenon actually is.

CLASSIFIED:
what institutions know.

PUBLIC:
what is released.

These may differ for legitimate uncertainty or security reasons.

## Spatial consequence

At FC-E1 or higher, survey maps must mark:
- evidence coordinate;
- exclusion radius;
- observation standoff;
- route to retreat/portal;
- destructive-sampling prohibition zone where applicable.

## Anti-war shortcut

First contact does not automatically imply hostility.

Evidence of weapons does not prove hostile intent.

Territorial disagreement does not by itself determine the later war cause.
