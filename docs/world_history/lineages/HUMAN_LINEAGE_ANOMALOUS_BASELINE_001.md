# HUMAN_LINEAGE_ANOMALOUS_BASELINE_001 — Natural Human Ability Heritage

Mechanism class: NATURAL_ABILITY

## Definition

This record describes the shared biological inheritance model for naturally occurring human ability users before and after Veinfall. It is not one family and does not imply that every ability user descends from one recent ancestor.

## Biological rules

1. Natural ability users are Homo sapiens.
2. A rare heritable phenotype increases the capacity to interact with anomalous physical phenomena.
3. The phenotype can be carried without obvious manifestation.
4. Inheritance is probabilistic, polygenic/epigenetic in presentation, and influenced by developmental conditions.
5. Exact abilities are not copied Mendelian traits.
6. Related people can manifest different abilities while sharing compatibility patterns.
7. A natural user normally has one underlying ability architecture.
8. Techniques are expressions of that architecture, not automatically separate abilities.
9. Severe mutation, artificial intervention, crystal exposure, or other external mechanisms may complicate the phenotype, but those cases must be recorded separately.

## Pre-Veinfall prevalence

Manifest active user:
- historical baseline estimate: approximately 1 per 5–20 million humans.

Latent compatibility:
- more common than manifest ability;
- exact rate UNKNOWN.

High-expression lineages:
- rare family clusters may produce users at rates far above global baseline;
- these clusters are candidates for HUMAN_LINEAGE_* child records.

## Scientific terminology

Working 2670 research term:
ANOMALOUS COUPLING PHENOTYPE (ACP)

ACP is a model, not proof of the ultimate origin of abilities.

Researchers use it to describe measurable correlations among:
- neural activation patterns;
- stress-linked expression;
- unusual metabolic demand in some powers;
- heritable compatibility markers;
- field interaction signatures;
- variable organ/tissue involvement by ability type.

No single anatomical "power organ" exists across all users.

## Natural versus crystal-induced

NATURAL_ABILITY:
- biologically integrated;
- can manifest without crystal implantation or equipment;
- usually develops with the person's body;
- inheritance can affect probability.

CRYSTAL_INDUCED_CAPABILITY:
- depends on crystal exposure, device, integration, treatment, engineered tissue, or another external interface;
- can imitate some natural effects;
- may impose toxicity, rejection, instability, dependency, or failure modes absent from a natural user's baseline.

These mechanisms may interact but must not be conflated.
