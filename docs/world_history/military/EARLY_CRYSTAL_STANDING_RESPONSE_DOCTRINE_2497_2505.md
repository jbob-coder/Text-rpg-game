## Transition

Emergency detachments evolve into standing anomaly-response formations.

## Typical formation components

- command;
- conventional security infantry;
- engineering;
- medical;
- hazardous-material/crystal handling;
- wildlife/anomaly ecology;
- logistics;
- communications;
- ability-user liaison/support where available.

## Mission families

- exclusion-zone security;
- recurring breach perimeter;
- dangerous-fauna response;
- evacuation;
- regulated crystal convoy protection;
- contaminated-site recovery;
- support to civilian authorities.

## Equipment

Predominantly conventional:
- rifles;
- protective clothing;
- respirators;
- armored/utility vehicles;
- field sensors;
- containment equipment;
- veterinary/medical kits.

Experimental crystal systems remain rare and specialist-supported.

## Doctrine lesson

Lethal force solves only part of an anomaly incident.

Commanders must consider:
- contamination;
- ecological consequences;
- civilian movement;
- specimen intelligence value;
- unknown abilities;
- infrastructure;
- secondary breach risk.

## Handoff

This standing-force model becomes the military baseline that Crystal Industrialization later equips more heavily.
