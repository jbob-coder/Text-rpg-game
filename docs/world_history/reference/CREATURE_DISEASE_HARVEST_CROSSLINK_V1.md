Status: ACTIVE_REFERENCE_WITH_DRAFT_CANON_DETAIL
Live-game clock effect: NONE

## Purpose

Cross-links creature ecology, carcass risk, crystal value, and harvest law.

## Species matrix

### SPECIES_SHARDTAIL_FORAGER_001
Disease role:
possible small-host vector for terrestrial/offworld-adapted parasites in mixed zones.

Harvest:
normally not economically targeted.

Law:
wildlife disease control more relevant than crystal license.

### SPECIES_GLASSFANG_STALKER_001
Disease role:
bite/wound contamination and parasite carriage.

Harvest:
limited specimen/research value.

Law:
regulated removal in protected ecological zones.

### SPECIES_IRONMANE_PACKRUNNER_001
Disease role:
pack disease can spread rapidly through territorial populations.

Harvest:
licensed where populations support it; carcass evidence required after defensive kill in regulated areas.

### SPECIES_STONECROWN_GRAZER_001
Disease role:
herd outbreaks can affect broad migration system.

Harvest:
strongly quota-sensitive because reproduction is slow.

Legal concern:
removal of breeding adults can trigger ecological offense in protected ranges.

### SPECIES_CROWNBACK_REAVER_001
Disease role:
offworld tissues may carry unknown biological agents.

Harvest:
high-value but tightly controlled in frontier jurisdictions.

Required:
quarantine, tissue screening, crystal chain of custody.

### SPECIES_SKYSAIL_HUNTER_001
Disease role:
aerial movement can spread parasites between distant nesting areas.

Harvest:
nest disturbance usually more restricted than removal of a single dangerous individual.

### SPECIES_MIRECOIL_FILTER_PREDATOR_001
Disease/toxin role:
tissue can concentrate waterborne toxins and anomalous particulates.

Harvest:
protective equipment mandatory in certified operations.

### SPECIES_VEINBORER_001
Disease role:
low direct infectious risk; burrows can move contaminated soil.

Harvest:
often secondary to infrastructure control.

## Crystal classification rule

Beast crystals remain SRC-BIO.

Yield descriptors must separately record:
- stability;
- output;
- bioactivity;
- hazard.

Threat tier does not determine crystal quality.

## Law rule

Relevant legal systems may include:
- LAW_BIOCRYSTAL_HARVEST_LICENSE_2527;
- protected-habitat rules;
- frontier quarantine;
- carcass evidence/chain of custody;
- local wildlife control law.

## Scenario consequence

After a creature is killed or captured, the event does not automatically end.

Possible consequences:
- carcass quarantine;
- ecological investigation;
- harvest licensing;
- evidence seizure;
- disease screening;
- compensation/property claim;
- population monitoring.
