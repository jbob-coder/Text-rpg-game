## SPECIES_SHARDTAIL_FORAGER_001

Common name:
Shardtail Forager

Origin:
ORIGIN_CRYSTALLIZED / ECO-CRYSTALIZED

Earth lineage:
small terrestrial omnivore/scavenger descendant; exact pre-Veinfall source population intentionally generalized.

Historical availability:
stable populations documented by 2480s.

Adult dimensions:
- body length: 0.42–0.58 m
- tail: 0.24–0.36 m
- shoulder height: 0.19–0.25 m
- body width: 0.14–0.20 m
- mass: 2.8–5.6 kg

Movement:
- sprint: short bursts;
- climb capable;
- typical turn envelope under 0.6 m.

Diet:
seeds, insects, fungi, carrion, crystal-adapted roots.

Trophic role:
small consumer + scavenger + seed disperser.

Reproduction:
2–4 litters/year where food is abundant; 3–7 young typical.

Threat:
T0 normally.
T1 in contaminated colonies or when disease-bearing.

Modifiers:
RAPID_REPRODUCTION, CRYSTAL_REACTIVE.

Abilities:
- CAB_CRYSTAL_TOXIN_SEQUESTRATION (limited).

Crystal yield:
small SRC-BIO deposits, usually O0–O1, highly variable.
Scientific/industrial value often exceeds raw energy value.

Ecological importance:
major carrion cleanup and seed dispersal.

Response:
do not eradicate casually.

Crystal yield:
small SRC-BIO, typically S1–S2 / O0–O1 / B1–B2.

Ironmane Packrunner

Origin:

stable documented lineage by early 2500s.

Adult dimensions:
- length: 1.55–1.95 m
- shoulder height: 0.78–0.98 m

Crystal yield:

## SPECIES_STONECROWN_GRAZER_001

Common name:
Stonecrown Grazer

Origin:
ORIGIN_CRYSTALLIZED

Role:
large defensive herbivore/megafauna.

Adult dimensions:
- body length: 4.6–5.8 m
- shoulder height: 2.35–2.85 m
- body width: 1.75–2.2 m
- mass: 3,200–5,400 kg

Movement:
- minimum practical turning diameter: 7–10 m
- cannot fit ordinary building interiors without structural damage.
- herd spacing: 4–12 m under calm movement.

Diet:
grasses, woody browse, crystal-adapted shrubs.

Threat:
T1 calm individual at distance.
T3 stampede/small herd near infrastructure.
T4 mass migration through dense settlement corridor.

Modifiers:
MIGRATORY, TERRITORIAL during breeding, STRUCTURE_BREAKER by mass.

Abilities:
- CAB_DERMAL_CRYSTAL_PLATE;
- CAB_THERMAL_DUMP.

Reproduction:
single calf after long gestation; slow population recovery.

Crystal yield:
larger SRC-BIO structures possible, but removal from reproductive adults is ecologically costly.

Harvest note:
protected in many recovery zones after overharvest lessons.

## SPECIES_CROWNBACK_REAVER_001

Common name:
Crownback Reaver

Origin:
ORIGIN_OFFWORLD / ECO-OFFWORLD

Known world:
DESTINATION_HAVEN_03 ecological region, not Earth-native.

Historical availability to humans:
documented after 2570s surveys.

Adult dimensions:
- body length: 5.8–7.4 m
- hip/shoulder height: 2.1–2.7 m
- body width: 1.9–2.5 m
- mass: 2,800–4,600 kg

Territory:
typically 45–110 km² depending prey density.

Movement:
- high-speed turn radius: 8–14 m
- enclosed human corridors generally unsuitable.

Diet:
large local herbivores and carrion.

Threat:
T3 single in open frontier.
T4 if established near settlement/portal route.

Modifiers:
TERRITORIAL, STEALTH in native brush, STRUCTURE_BREAKER.

Abilities:
- CAB_RESonance_SENSE;
- CAB_BURST_REGENERATION, limited.

Regeneration constraint:
cannot replace major organ loss rapidly; heavy metabolic cost.

Crystal yield:
SRC-BIO / S1–S3 variable / O1–O3 / B2–B3.
High market interest creates poaching pressure.

## SPECIES_VEINBORER_001

Common name:
Veinborer

Origin:
ORIGIN_CRYSTALLIZED

Known habitat:
Earth mineral/crystalized badlands and some industrially disturbed subsurface zones.

Adult dimensions:
- length: 1.8–2.6 m
- body diameter: 0.44–0.68 m
- mass: 110–210 kg

Burrow:
- normal tunnel: 0.7–1.1 m diameter
- major den chamber: 3–7 m across
- local burrow network may extend 0.5–3.0 km.

Diet:
roots, fungi, invertebrates, mineral-associated microbial mats; opportunistic scavenging.

Threat:
T1 individual.
T2 near foundations/utility corridors.
T3 colony undermining critical infrastructure.

Modifiers:
BURROWING, STRUCTURE_BREAKER by undermining.

Abilities:
- CAB_BURROW_RESONANCE_MAPPING.

Crystal yield:
small-to-moderate mineralized plates, often contaminated by soil/metal.

Ecological role:
soil turnover and underground nutrient transport.
