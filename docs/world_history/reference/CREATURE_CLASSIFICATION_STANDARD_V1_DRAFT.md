Purpose: operational creature classification for history and scenario selection

## Principle

Threat is not equivalent to physical strength.

Operational threat combines:
- lethality;
- durability;
- mobility;
- stealth;
- intelligence;
- group size;
- reproduction;
- spread;
- toxin/disease potential;
- anomalous abilities;
- infrastructure damage;
- resistance to ordinary response;
- strategic consequences.

## Origin classes

- ORIGIN_NATIVE — ordinary terrestrial lineage.
- ORIGIN_ALTERED — terrestrial lineage changed by crystal exposure.
- ORIGIN_CRYSTALLIZED — stable heritable crystal-adapted lineage.
- ORIGIN_OFFWORLD — non-Earth lineage introduced through portal access.
- ORIGIN_HYBRID — mixed, symbiotic, introgressed, or composite lineage.
- ORIGIN_ENGINEERED — intentionally created or extensively modified.
- ORIGIN_UNKNOWN — evidence insufficient.

A species can change threat level by scale.

## Draft threat classes

### T0 — MONITORED
Ordinary or anomalous organism with low direct danger under normal conditions.

### T1 — LOCAL HAZARD
Can seriously injure or kill an unprepared person but is manageable by informed civilians or ordinary local response.

### T2 — SPECIALIST HAZARD
Requires trained responders, specialized equipment, or controlled tactics.

### T3 — SETTLEMENT THREAT
Can overwhelm ordinary local response or threaten a neighborhood, facility, small settlement, or critical route.

### T4 — REGIONAL THREAT
Can disrupt cities, regions, ecosystems, or major infrastructure and requires coordinated military/civil response.

### T5 — STRATEGIC THREAT
Can threaten national or interworld strategic systems, portal networks, large populations, or multiple regions.

### T6 — CIVILIZATION THREAT
Credible potential for civilization-scale destabilization if uncontrolled.

### TX — UNBOUNDED
Insufficiently understood, nonlinear, or context-dependent threat that cannot be responsibly assigned a normal tier.

## Response doctrine

Every species record must state the lowest response capability considered reasonable:
- civilian avoidance;
- local animal control;
- police/emergency;
- licensed specialist;
- Academy-trained field team;
- military tactical unit;
- strategic command;
- quarantine/evacuation only.

## Ecology gate

No creature entry may be promoted without:
- diet;
- trophic role;
- reproduction;
- habitat;
- population constraint;
- predator/prey relation;
- historical range;
- portal/crystal relation where applicable.

## Scenario gate

A creature is eligible for a scenario only when:
YEAR + WORLD + REGION + BIOME + THREAT + KNOWLEDGE + RESPONSE_CAPABILITY are compatible.

This standard remains DRAFT until tested against multiple complete species records.
