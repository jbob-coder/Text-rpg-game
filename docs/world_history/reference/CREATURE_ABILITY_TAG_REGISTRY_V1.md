# CREATURE_ABILITY_TAG_REGISTRY_V1

Status: ACTIVE_REFERENCE_STANDARD_WITH_DRAFT_CANON_ENTRIES
Live-game clock effect: NONE

## Purpose

Provides reusable biological/anomalous ability tags for creature records.

These are not human ability slots and do not imply Steal compatibility.

## Tags

### CAB_RESonance_SENSE
Detects crystal/field activity beyond ordinary sensory channels.

Constraints:
range depends on environment and signal strength.

### CAB_DERMAL_CRYSTAL_PLATE
Crystal-associated dermal or subdermal reinforcement.

Costs:
mass, growth resources, reduced flexibility, shedding/injury risk.

### CAB_THERMAL_DUMP
Rapidly dissipates or redirects body heat.

Use:
survival in heat/high-output metabolic states.

### CAB_FIELD_CAMOUFLAGE
Reduces detectability to selected field/resonance sensors.

Does not equal invisibility.

### CAB_BURST_REGENERATION
Accelerated repair after injury.

Costs:
extreme energy/protein demand; limited by tissue loss and metabolic reserves.

### CAB_SHOCK_DISCHARGE
Short-range electrical/crystal-associated discharge.

Requires:
charge accumulation and recovery period.

### CAB_PACK_RESONANCE_CUE
Pack members coordinate using low-intensity field/vocal/chemical cues.

Not telepathy.

### CAB_PRESSURE_TOLERANCE
Specialized anatomy tolerating large pressure changes.

### CAB_BURROW_RESONANCE_MAPPING
Uses vibration/field response to map subsurface voids and dense crystal seams.

### CAB_GLIDE_FIELD_STABILIZATION
Aerial species uses field-responsive tissues to improve stability/lift efficiency.

Does not negate gravity.

### CAB_CRYSTAL_TOXIN_SEQUESTRATION
Stores otherwise harmful compounds/crystal particulates in protected tissue.

May make carcass hazardous.

### CAB_HARDENED_CORE_SHIELDING
Dense tissue protects internal crystal organ/core from ordinary trauma.

## Record rule

Every ability tag must specify:
- biological mechanism or UNKNOWN;
- activation conditions;
- effective range;
- metabolic cost;
- cooldown/recovery if applicable;
- known counters;
- failure conditions;
- historical availability.

## Anti-drift

Do not infer:
- human ability copyability;
- XP;
- ability-book compatibility;
- market price;
- Steal interaction

from creature ability tags.
