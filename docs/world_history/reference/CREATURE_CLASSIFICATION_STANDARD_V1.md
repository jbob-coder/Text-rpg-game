## Principle

Threat is not equivalent to physical strength.

Operational threat combines:
- lethality;
- durability;
- mobility;
- stealth;
- intelligence;
- group size;
- reproduction;
- spread;
- toxin/disease potential;
- anomalous abilities;
- infrastructure damage;
- resistance to ordinary response;
- strategic consequences.

## Origin classes

- ORIGIN_NATIVE — ordinary terrestrial lineage.
- ORIGIN_ALTERED — terrestrial lineage altered by crystal exposure.
- ORIGIN_CRYSTALLIZED — stable heritable crystal-adapted lineage.
- ORIGIN_OFFWORLD — non-Earth lineage introduced through portal access.
- ORIGIN_HYBRID — mixed, symbiotic, introgressed, or composite lineage.
- ORIGIN_ENGINEERED — intentionally created or extensively modified.
- ORIGIN_UNKNOWN — evidence insufficient.

Ecological mapping:
ORIGIN_NATIVE ~= ECO-NATIVE
ORIGIN_ALTERED ~= ECO-ALTERED
ORIGIN_CRYSTALLIZED ~= ECO-CRYSTALIZED
ORIGIN_OFFWORLD ~= ECO-OFFWORLD
ORIGIN_HYBRID ~= ECO-HYBRID
ORIGIN_ENGINEERED ~= ECO-ENGINEERED.

## Threat classes

### T0 — MONITORED
Low direct danger under normal conditions.

Typical response:
civilian awareness, wildlife management, routine monitoring.

### T1 — LOCAL HAZARD
Can seriously injure or kill an unprepared person but is manageable by informed civilians or ordinary local response.

Typical response:
avoidance, local animal control, police/emergency depending context.

### T2 — SPECIALIST HAZARD
Requires trained responders, specialized equipment, controlled tactics, or environmental protection.

Typical response:
licensed specialist or Academy-trained field team.

### T3 — SETTLEMENT THREAT
Can overwhelm ordinary local response or threaten a neighborhood, facility, small settlement, or critical route.

Typical response:
multiple specialist teams, emergency coordination, possible tactical unit.

### T4 — REGIONAL THREAT
Can disrupt cities, regions, ecosystems, or major infrastructure.

Typical response:
coordinated military/civil response, evacuation, environmental containment.

### T5 — STRATEGIC THREAT
Can threaten portal networks, major population centers, national/interworld infrastructure, or multiple regions.

Typical response:
strategic command and multi-jurisdiction response.

### T6 — CIVILIZATION THREAT
Credible potential for civilization-scale destabilization if uncontrolled.

Use extremely rarely.

### TX — UNBOUNDED
Threat cannot be responsibly bounded because behavior is unknown, nonlinear, or context-dependent.

TX is not automatically stronger than T6.

## Physical-fit requirement

Every creature record must include:
- adult body length;
- standing/body height;
- body width;
- mass range;
- turning radius or maneuver envelope;
- doorway/corridor compatibility where relevant;
- jump/climb/swim/burrow envelope where relevant;
- group spacing.

A creature is not eligible for a scenario merely because the threat tier matches.

The encounter space must physically fit the body and behavior.

## Ecology gate

No creature entry may be promoted without:
- diet;
- trophic role;
- reproduction;
- juvenile stage;
- habitat;
- population constraint;
- predator/prey relation;
- historical range;
- portal/crystal relation where applicable.
