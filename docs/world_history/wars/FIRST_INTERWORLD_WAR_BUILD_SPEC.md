# FIRST_INTERWORLD_WAR_BUILD_SPEC

Status: ACTIVE_BUILD_PLAN
Authority: ROUNDS_085_090
Target event: WAR_FIRST_INTERWORLD_2645_2659
Hard constraint: NOT CAUSED BY HOMUNCULUS NETWORK

## Prerequisites

Do not write the war as final canon until:
- Kharvori biology/civilization exists;
- human 2606–2644 frontier history exists;
- first-contact chronology exists;
- early diplomacy/trade exists;
- border/resource disputes exist;
- military capabilities and portal logistics are bounded.

## Causal chain requirements

The war must emerge from:
RESOURCE/TERRITORIAL PRESSURE
-> SECURITY DILEMMA
-> COMPETING CLAIMS
-> INCIDENTS
-> FAILED DIPLOMACY
-> MOBILIZATION
-> SPECIFIC TRIGGER
-> WAR.

No single unexplained villain action may substitute for the chain.

## Required war design

- participating human polities;
- participating Kharvori factions;
- war aims;
- mobilization systems;
- portal corridors;
- theaters;
- supply;
- intelligence;
- propaganda;
- civilian evacuation;
- occupation;
- prisoners;
- disease;
- beast/ecology effects;
- technology adaptation;
- economic rationing;
- Academy/predecessor role;
- collaboration cases;
- internal dissent;
- peace factions;
- major campaigns;
- turning points;
- casualty methodology;
- failed negotiations;
- exhaustion;
- final negotiation incentives.

## Fourteen-year duration test

The history must explain why:
- portals make fronts discontinuous;
- routes are defendable bottlenecks;
- industrial replacement takes time;
- both sides adapt;
- neither side can easily reach or occupy all strategic depth;
- political limits prevent simple annihilation;
- civilian/economic exhaustion accumulates.

## End-state requirement

EVENT_HALCYON_GATE_CONCORD_2660 must be a negotiated response to incentives and limits established by the war, not an arbitrary peace button.
