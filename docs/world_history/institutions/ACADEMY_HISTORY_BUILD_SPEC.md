It is not merely a game hub.

- military education;
- natural-ability research/training;
- emergency response;
- beast ecology/response;
- portal safety;
- crystal handling;
- field medicine;
- civil defense;
- wartime officer/specialist training.

## Historical questions

For each era:
- Who trains dangerous/rare ability users?
- Who teaches beast response?
- Who controls portal access?
- Who certifies crystal handling?
- When do these systems merge?
- Which parts stay civilian?
- Which become military?
- What changed during the First Interworld War?
- What changed after 2660?
