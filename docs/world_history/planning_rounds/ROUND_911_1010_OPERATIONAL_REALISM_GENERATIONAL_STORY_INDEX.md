## Purpose

Convert the historical world into operationally believable everyday systems.

This cycle asks what happens between major events:
- where food comes from;
- how power and water stay on;
- how information moves;
- who repairs equipment;
- how evidence and courts work;
- how NPCs live normal weeks;
- whether creatures physically fit scenes;
- what happens after dangerous incidents;
- how the Academy functions through an entire year.

Every applicable block must include historical context plus coordinates, dimensions, capacity, flow, or range.
