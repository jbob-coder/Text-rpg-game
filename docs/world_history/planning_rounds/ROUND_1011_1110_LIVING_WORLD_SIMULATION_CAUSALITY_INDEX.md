## Purpose

Convert the completed history/reference layers into a persistent living-world simulation model.

This cycle focuses on what changes when the player does nothing:
- weather changes;
- people age;
- workers change shifts;
- supplies move;
- prices react;
- creatures migrate;
- settlements grow or shrink;
- institutions spend resources;
- rumors spread;
- injuries heal or worsen;
- births and deaths change households;
- disasters propagate through infrastructure.

Every applicable block must include:
TIME + LOCATION + CAPACITY + CAUSAL STATE + HISTORICAL VERSION.
