## Spatial rule

Every entity that occupies physical space must include:
- frame;
- parent;
- coordinates or explicit UNKNOWN/CLASSIFIED;
- orientation where relevant;
- dimensions;
- movement/access envelope;
- temporal validity;
- uncertainty.

## Story rule

Reference records must include enough lived historical context to explain why the thing exists.

The encyclopedia may not become a context-free stat catalog.
