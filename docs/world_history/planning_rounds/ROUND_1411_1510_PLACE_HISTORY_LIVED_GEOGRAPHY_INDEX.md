## Purpose

Continue the world-history project by turning major locations into **historical organisms** rather than static map pins.

This cycle documents how places:
- are founded;
- grow;
- change ownership;
- suffer disaster;
- rebuild;
- acquire new roads/utilities;
- lose old functions;
- become ruins;
- become memorials;
- become militarized;
- become ordinary again.

Every block combines:
- readable story/history;
- spatial coordinates;
- dimensions;
- temporal versions;
- access and transport;
- population/capacity;
- ecology;
- infrastructure;
- legal/political context;
- PUBLIC / CLASSIFIED / TRUE knowledge.

The cycle must not advance Jack or Elias live gameplay.

## Place-history invariant

Every persistent place promoted in this cycle should include:
- stable place ID;
- parent spatial ID;
- world/body;
- frame;
- coordinate;
- uncertainty;
- valid_from;
- valid_to;
- founding date or earliest confirmed use;
- original footprint;
- later footprints;
- population/capacity by era where relevant;
- building/route additions;
- destroyed/abandoned zones;
- utility systems;
- ecological context;
- access/egress;
- ownership/jurisdiction history;
- major historical events;
- public memory;
- classified/true divergence where relevant.

## Story invariant

Each block must define at least one narrative/microhistory that makes a location feel lived in.

A place-story should answer:
- who wakes up there;
- who works there;
- what is sold, repaired, taught, hidden, feared, or celebrated;
- what changed after a historical event;
- which physical spaces carry memory;
- what a visitor in 2670 can still see.

## Coordinate/dimension rule
