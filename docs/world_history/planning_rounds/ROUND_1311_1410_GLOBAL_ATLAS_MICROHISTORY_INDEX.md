## Purpose

Continue the world-history project as an author-facing **global atlas + microhistory** cycle.

This cycle is designed to make the setting usable at scene scale without replacing the larger chronological books.

Every block combines:
- readable historical or lived-world story material;
- persistent spatial records;
- coordinates or explicit UNKNOWN/CLASSIFIED anchors;
- dimensions and capacities;
- temporal versions;
- ecology and infrastructure compatibility;
- public/classified/true knowledge separation.

The cycle must not advance Jack or Elias live gameplay.

- 1311–1320 — Temporal spatial versioning and microhistory compiler.
- 1321–1330 — Climate, weather, season, and environmental-hazard geography.

- 1341–1350 — Rural/frontier settlements, farms, wilderness edges, and regional ecology.

## Story rule

Each block must produce at least one lived-world narrative target answering:
- who uses this place/system;
- what a normal day looks like;
- what failure looks like;
- how history changed the space;
- what people in that era believed;
- what later historians know that contemporary people did not.

Narrative examples must remain separate from protagonist-controlled scenes.

## Spatial rule

Every persistent spatial record should carry, where applicable:
- stable ID;
- world/body ID;
- parent spatial ID;
- frame ID;
- latitude/longitude or local Cartesian coordinates;
- elevation/depth;
- orientation;
- uncertainty class;
- valid_from / valid_to;
- length × width × height;
- footprint/area;
- capacity;
- access/egress;
- movement clearance;
- service/support spaces;
- hazard/exclusion envelope;
- historical revisions.

## Historical-version rule

A location may have multiple valid versions.

Examples:
- pre-Veinfall;
- Veinfall emergency version;
- Early Crystal rebuild;
- industrial expansion;
- wartime hardened version;
- 2670 reconstruction version.

Do not silently reuse one era's footprint in another.

## Coordinate rule

Exact coordinates are allowed only when:
- the location is author-created and intended to be spatially fixed; or
- existing canon already fixes the location.

Otherwise use:
- SPATIAL_AUTHORITATIVE_APPROX;
- SPATIAL_HISTORICAL_ESTIMATE;
- SPATIAL_CLASSIFIED;
- SPATIAL_UNKNOWN.

No false precision.

## Protagonist-mystery rule

This cycle may map:
- public Academy spaces;
- world infrastructure;
- generic dorm/class/training archetypes;
- historical routes.

It must not establish:
- Jack's hidden family sites;
- Elias's hidden family sites;
- secret origin laboratories for Steal or Perfect Clone;
- protagonist-specific rooms/routes not already canon.
