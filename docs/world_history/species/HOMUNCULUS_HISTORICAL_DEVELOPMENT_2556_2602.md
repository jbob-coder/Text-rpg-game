# Homunculus Historical Development — 2556–2602

## Purpose

This file expands the already-established origin, rebellion, and enslavement history without changing the core species authority.

## 2556–2561 — Precursor programs

Human medical, military, mining, and frontier agencies fund increasingly aggressive crystal-compatible tissue research.

Goals include:
- radiation/toxin tolerance;
- improved injury recovery;
- enhanced senses;
- heavy-labor adaptation;
- survival in hostile portal environments;
- biologically integrated crystal handling.

The programs use:
- engineered human-compatible tissue scaffolds;
- beast-derived traits;
- crystal-mediated stabilization;
- controlled developmental environments.

At this stage, researchers are not publicly creating a new people.

## EVENT_HOMUNCULUS_FIRST_VIABLE_2563

Status: DRAFT_CANON / CLASSIFIED_TRUE

The first organism later recognized as a true viable homunculus survives long-term development with:
- humanlike cognition;
- stable beast-derived traits;
- crystal-adapted tissues;
- language-learning capacity;
- self-directed problem solving.

Researchers initially classify the subject as an engineered biological platform rather than a legal person.

## EVENT_HOMUNCULUS_SAPIENCE_CONFIRMED_2565

Multiple programs independently document:
- autobiographical memory;
- future planning;
- deception;
- empathy;
- fear of death;
- attachment;
- negotiation;
- moral reasoning.

CLASSIFIED_TRUE:
By 2565 there is sufficient evidence for senior researchers to know at least some homunculi are fully sapient.

Publicly, they remain described as advanced engineered organisms.

## 2566–2573 — Controlled production

Programs expand because homunculi can perform dangerous work in:
- portal facilities;
- contaminated extraction zones;
- crystal refineries;
- military logistics;
- hazardous construction.

Institutional doctrine frames them as manufactured assets.

Common controls:
- numbered identity;
- restricted movement;
- reproductive prohibition;
- command conditioning;
- surveillance;
- ownership contracts;
- termination authority.

Treatment varies by program.

## EVENT_HOMUNCULUS_REPRODUCTIVE_LINEAGE_2574

Status: DRAFT_CANON / CLASSIFIED_TRUE

A stable homunculus pair produces viable offspring without laboratory gestation.

Historical consequence:
The claim that homunculi are merely individually manufactured devices becomes biologically and ethically harder to defend.

Authorities suppress broad publication.

## 2575–2586 — Expansion and coercive labor

Reproducing lineages and scalable developmental programs increase population.

Homunculi become economically important in:
- frontier construction;
- mining;
- military support;
- waste handling;
- beast-management;
- high-risk agriculture;
- crystal processing.

Resistance also expands.

Forms of resistance include:
- work slowdown;
- sabotage;
- hidden education;
- escape;
- falsified production records;
- protection of children;
- clandestine communication between facilities.

## EVENT_HOMUNCULUS_RECALL_ORDER_2587

Status: DRAFT_CANON / CLASSIFIED_TRUE

A coordinated security directive orders detention, reproductive separation, and destruction/termination of selected high-risk homunculus populations after a series of facility uprisings and intelligence leaks.

This order is not the sole cause of the rebellion, but it becomes the immediate trigger for coordinated armed resistance.

## EVENT_HOMUNCULUS_REBELLION_2587_2601

Preserved canonical ID and date range.

Conflict development:
- 2587–2589: coordinated escapes, facility seizures, sabotage;
- 2590–2594: territorial rebel enclaves and organized military formations;
- 2595–2598: peak conflict, attacks on portal/logistics infrastructure, human counteroffensives;
- 2599–2601: fragmentation, sieges, defeat of major rebel commands.

The rebellion is not one ideology.

Homunculus factions include:
- emancipation coalitions;
- separatists;
- retaliatory militants;
- survival-focused communities;
- negotiated-surrender factions;
- groups seeking coexistence;
- authoritarian rebel commands.

Human factions include:
- extermination advocates;
- security hardliners;
- economic owners;
- abolitionists;
- researchers attempting legal recognition;
- military pragmatists;
- civilians primarily concerned with survival.

## Violence and responsibility

TRUE layer:
- human coercion, experimentation, ownership, reproductive control, and recall/termination policy are major causal factors;
- rebel groups also commit killings, kidnappings, sabotage, and attacks affecting civilians;
- neither species acts as a single moral bloc.

This historical structure must not be simplified into either:
"humans were innocent and betrayed"
or
"all rebel violence was justified."

## 2602 aftermath

LAW_HOMUNCULUS_CUSTODIAL_ORDER_2602 becomes the dominant human response after organized rebel defeat.

The law institutionalizes:
- noncitizen status;
- coerced labor;
- movement control;
- ownership/custodial transfer;
- inherited legal classification.

In-world bureaucracies may use euphemisms, but the actual social institution is slavery.

## Present-era consequences

By 2670:
- rebellion memory remains politically powerful;
- official education emphasizes homunculus violence;
- classified archives contain evidence of pre-rebellion sapience recognition and coercive policy;
- free/fugitive communities preserve different histories;
- family memories can conflict with official narratives.
