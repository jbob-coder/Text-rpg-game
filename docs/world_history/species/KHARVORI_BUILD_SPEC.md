# KHARVORI_BUILD_SPEC

Status: ACTIVE_BUILD_PLAN
Authority: ROUNDS_081_084
Target phase: PHASE 11
War dependency: MUST COMPLETE BEFORE WAR_FIRST_INTERWORLD_2645_2659

## Principle

Kharvori history must exist before humans discover it.

Do not construct them only as an enemy army.

## Required biology

- body plan;
- sensory systems;
- reproduction;
- lifespan;
- development;
- diet;
- disease;
- environmental tolerances;
- internal biological diversity;
- relationship to crystals;
- relationship to portals;
- natural abilities if any.

## Required home ecology

- climate;
- geography;
- producers;
- prey/herbivores;
- predators;
- pathogens;
- domesticated species;
- strategic resources;
- ecological hazards;
- settlement constraints.

## Required civilization

- political systems;
- family/kinship;
- education;
- law;
- economy;
- labor;
- religion/philosophy;
- language/communication;
- art;
- status;
- crime;
- diplomacy;
- internal minorities;
- competing factions.

## Required technology

- energy;
- materials;
- medicine;
- transport;
- communication;
- portal capability;
- weapons;
- defense;
- logistics;
- industrial limits.

## Required pre-contact chronology

Build at least:
- long-term civilization history;
- major internal conflicts;
- prior portal experience;
- first evidence of human activity;
- debate over humans;
- contact preparations;
- internal factions favoring trade, containment, expansion, war, or isolation.

## Anti-monolith rule

Kharvori individuals and factions must disagree.

The war later emerges from institutions and pressures, not an entire species sharing one mind.
