Live-game clock effect: NONE

## Core rule

This book is not a thin chronology and is not the technical encyclopedia.

It explains how people lived through history:
- what they believed;
- what they feared;
- what changed around them;
- what institutions did;
- how daily life adapted;
- how ecology, technology, law, war, religion, economy, and culture interacted.

Technical facts that need precise lookup belong in the reference encyclopedia and are cross-linked from chapters.

## Knowledge safety

A chapter may contain:
- PUBLIC account;
- CLASSIFIED account;
- TRUE author-history;
- PROTAGONIST_MYSTERY boundary.

The presence of TRUE material in this author book does not grant it to Jack, Elias, NPCs, or players in-character.

PROTAGONIST_MYSTERY material may document the existence of a gap but must not resolve the gap.
