## Review principle

Knowledge of an exercise/training method is not the same as knowledge of:
- exact hidden Status qualification;
- passive name;
- deterministic threshold;
- internal progress;
- resulting numerical modifier.

Ordinary movement science can be public while the Status unlock rule remains hidden.

# Safety/anti-exploit rule

Movement knowledge must never make deliberate injury or uncontrolled risk the optimal unlock strategy. Public rumors that encourage such behavior should be explicitly identified as false and countered by in-world legitimate training doctrine.
