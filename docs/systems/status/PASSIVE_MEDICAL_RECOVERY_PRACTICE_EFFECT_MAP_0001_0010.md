## Family guardrails

- Medical passives do not create medical knowledge the character never learned.
- They do not reveal hidden diagnoses automatically.
- Treatment eligibility, tools, supplies, condition severity, and patient state remain authoritative.
- These passives do not guarantee recovery.
- Medication Discipline does not authorize a medication/procedure by itself.
- Rehabilitation Insight does not replace professional scope, protocol, or patient-specific limits.
- Same-resolver effects require capped composition.
- Duplicate ownership never stacks.
