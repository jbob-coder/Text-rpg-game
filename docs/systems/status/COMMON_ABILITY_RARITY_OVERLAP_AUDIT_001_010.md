## 1. Audit standard

A Common proposal should normally have:
- a narrow or practical governing law;
- predictable manifestation;
- clear environmental/physical constraints;
- established-looking counterplay;
- useful civilian/professional applications;
- a technique space that deepens execution without silently adding a second law.

This audit does **not** set final numeric balance or historical frequency.

## 3. ABILITY_COM_001 — Kinetic Palm

### Distinct law
Kinetic Palm creates a brief hand-origin impulse at touch/near-contact range.

### Adjacent overlap
- `ABILITY_UNC_001 Vector Nudge` alters an **already moving** object's velocity.
- `ABILITY_RAR_008 Momentum Bank` absorbs and later releases kinetic energy.

The conceptual separation is valid:
- Kinetic Palm can initiate a short impulse but is range/contact constrained;
- Vector Nudge should gain its Uncommon identity from non-contact or superior moving-target vector control, not simply from being another push;
- Momentum Bank has a storage/release cycle unavailable to Kinetic Palm.

### Risk
Until Vector Nudge receives a detailed range/precision packet, a highly mastered Kinetic Palm precision technique could intrude on its niche.

### Required resolution
When Uncommon 001 is deep-authored, explicitly compare:
- range;
- moving-target speed tolerance;
- mass ceiling;
- angular correction precision;
- ability to affect projectiles;
- resource efficiency.

Result: **PARTIAL — retain both; require adjacency calibration.**

## 4. ABILITY_COM_002 — Thermal Sight

Thermal Sight is sensory-only.

Adjacent higher-tier thermal abilities:
- `ABILITY_UNC_002 Heat Shaping` redistributes heat;
- `ABILITY_RAR_004 Cryo Sink` extracts/stores heat.

There is a clean perception-versus-manipulation distinction. Diagnostic Sweep remains legal only if it returns patterns rather than automatic diagnoses or heat control.

Result: **PASS.**

## 5. ABILITY_COM_003 — Grip Field

Grip Field modifies friction only at active user contact surfaces.

Potential conceptual neighbors:
- `ABILITY_UNC_009 Weight Shift` changes self-weight;
- `ABILITY_UNC_006 Magnetic Grip` affects ferromagnetic objects at range.

The mechanics can produce superficially similar outcomes such as better climbing or object retention, but they use different laws and counters.

Guardrail:
- Grip Field must never become remote friction editing;
- high friction must not reinforce the contacted material or user's joints.

Result: **PASS.**

## 6. ABILITY_COM_004 — Skin Reinforcement

Skin Reinforcement raises hardness/tensile strength of outer tissue.

Adjacent biological abilities:
- `ABILITY_UNC_003 Stonehide` mineralizes skin into armor;
- `ABILITY_EPI_003 Adaptive Arsenal` transforms tissues into learned biological tools/armor/weapons.

The law separation is valid, but the current Common advanced technique `Full Brace` could visually/mechanically approach Stonehide unless the protection bands differ clearly.

Required future distinction:
- Skin Reinforcement: flexible biological strengthening with limited coverage/output and no material conversion;
- Stonehide: actual mineralization, higher armor ceiling, larger weight/stiffness burden;
- Adaptive Arsenal: template-based structural transformation far beyond simple reinforcement.

Result: **PARTIAL — numeric/material protection band required.**

## 7. ABILITY_COM_005 — Lumen Pulse

Lumen Pulse emits visible light.

Adjacent:
- `ABILITY_RAR_009 Umbra Veil` redirects visible light around a bounded area.

Generation versus redirection is clean. The Common techniques remain illumination/signaling/dazzling applications and do not create invisibility, heat beams, or hard-light structures.

Result: **PASS.**

## 8. ABILITY_COM_006 — Static Reservoir

Static Reservoir stores bounded charge and releases it by contact/short arc.

Adjacent:
- `ABILITY_RAR_003 Lightning Conduit` channels and redirects high-voltage current through chosen conductive paths.

The intended distinction is valid:
- Common = limited personal reservoir, short discharge, strong environmental dependence;
- Rare = high-voltage routing/control with strategic electrical applications.

Risk:
Without numeric storage, discharge, and arc limits, a highly charged Common user could be authored like Lightning Conduit.

Required future distinction:
- maximum stored energy band;
- maximum safe/unsafe discharge band;
- arc distance;
- recharge sources;
- path-control complexity;
- whether Lightning Conduit can route external currents without storing them first.

Result: **PARTIAL — electrical bands required.**

## 9. ABILITY_COM_007 — Echo Map

Echo Map emits sound and interprets echoes.

Adjacent:
- `ABILITY_UNC_007 Sound Bend` manipulates sound propagation;
- `ABILITY_UNC_004 Motion Trace` senses residual movement traces.

Echo Map is physically constrained acoustic sensing and does not redirect/dampen sound or read abstract spatial residue.

Result: **PASS.**

## 10. ABILITY_COM_008 — Water Draw

Water Draw moves existing liquid water.

Adjacent:
- `ABILITY_UNC_008 Vapor Sculpt` shapes mist/steam/suspended water vapor.

The phase/domain boundary is clear, but rarity superiority is not yet self-evident because liquid-water mass movement can be more forceful than vapor shaping.

The Uncommon justification should come from one or more explicitly authored dimensions such as:
- broader volumetric shaping;
- concealment/sensory control;
- distributed multi-point control;
- phase-transition interaction only if later canon approves it;
- higher precision/technique depth;
- lower dependence on contiguous liquid bodies.

Do **not** simply raise Vapor Sculpt's rarity because vapor sounds more exotic.

Result: **PARTIAL — rarity rationale needs adjacency proof.**

## 11. ABILITY_COM_009 — Impact Cushion

Impact Cushion dissipates part of sudden impacts into lower-risk heat/vibration.

Adjacent:
- `ABILITY_RAR_008 Momentum Bank` absorbs kinetic energy and stores it for controlled later release.

Separation is valid only if Impact Cushion permanently dissipates/loses usable energy rather than secretly storing it.

Hard guardrail:
- Impact Cushion cannot accumulate offensive kinetic reserve;
- saturation/recovery remains a limiting state;
- internal injury and non-impact damage remain possible.

Result: **PARTIAL — retain strict dissipation-versus-storage distinction.**

## 12. ABILITY_COM_010 — Thread Command

Thread Command manipulates qualifying flexible low-mass lines.

Potential neighbors:
- `ABILITY_UNC_006 Magnetic Grip` controls ferromagnetic matter;
- future general telekinetic abilities, if authored.

The narrow material-domain restriction is strong enough for Common classification while leaving substantial mastery depth.

Hard guardrails:
- no invisible force acting independently of a line;
- no arbitrary rigid-object movement;
- line tensile strength and anchors remain physical;
- attached objects move only through actual transmitted line force.

Result: **PASS.**

## 13. Cross-Common duplication audit

No pair among Common 001–010 currently duplicates the same core law.

Closest relationships:
- Kinetic Palm and Impact Cushion both touch kinetic phenomena, but one generates impulse and one dissipates incoming impact.
- Thermal Sight and Echo Map are both sensory, but use different physical signals.
- Grip Field and Thread Command can both aid climbing/rigging, but use different laws.
- Skin Reinforcement and Impact Cushion are both defensive, but resolve different damage/force mechanisms.

Result: **PASS at law level.**

## 14. Rarity-drift guardrails for future authoring

Do not allow Common mastery to erase the rarity ladder by:
- giving Kinetic Palm remote free-form force control;
- giving Thermal Sight omniscient/x-ray sensing;
- letting Grip Field alter remote surfaces;
- making Skin Reinforcement equivalent to material transformation;
- expanding Lumen Pulse into arbitrary photonic constructs;
- allowing Static Reservoir unlimited generation or long-range lightning;
- turning Echo Map into silent perfect wall vision;
- allowing Water Draw to create water or control all fluids;
- letting Impact Cushion store/release attacks;
- turning Thread Command into general telekinesis.

Common abilities can become elite through Level, mastery, passives, tactics, equipment, and experience without acquiring higher-tier laws.

## 15. Numeric calibration blockers

The following remain intentionally unresolved and block final rarity certification:
- range bands;
- mass/force bands;
- duration;
- resource cost;
- recovery;
- simultaneous-effect count;
- output ceilings;
- mastery efficiency;
- exact occurrence frequency.

Do not invent these values inside runtime code before design calibration.

The first ten Common abilities are structurally coherent enough to continue refinement.

No ability should be deleted at this stage.

3. Static Reservoir versus Lightning Conduit;
4. Water Draw versus Vapor Sculpt;
