## 1. Gravity Well

Approved conceptual distinction:
- Weight Shift changes only the user's effective weight.
- Gravity Well creates an external localized field.

Guardrails:
- finite radius and duration;
- no negative/reversed gravity;
- no singularity;
- no unrestricted field sculpting;
- no immunity to secondary debris/movement.

Higher-tier boundary:
If later abilities manipulate mass sign, gravity direction, planetary-scale fields, or persistent fields, those remain categorically above this record.

Result: **PASS WITH GUARDRAIL.**

## 2. Phase Edge

Distinct law:
A thin phase boundary interacts with matter; the user does not become intangible.

Guardrails:
- thin geometry;
- bounded penetration;
- material/stability limits;
- no full-body phasing;
- no large-volume deletion;
- no permanent spatial cut.

Result: **PASS WITH GUARDRAIL.**

## 3. Lightning Conduit

Resolved lower-tier boundary:
- Static Reservoir = limited personal stored charge + contact/short arc.
- Lightning Conduit = routing substantial existing electrical energy through selected conductive paths.

Rare superiority comes from:
- throughput;
- routing;
- branching;
- infrastructure interaction.

It does not come from free energy.

Result: **PASS.**

## 4. Cryo Sink

Resolved lower-tier boundary:
- Heat Shaping = external heat redistribution.
- Cryo Sink = strong one-sided heat extraction into a personal thermal reserve.

Hard guardrails:
- finite reserve;
- no free cold;
- no automatic temperature immunity;
- no arbitrary energy conversion unless separately authored.

Result: **PASS.**

## 5. Bioelectric Overdrive

Overlap review:
- passive physical conditioning improves ordinary capacity/efficiency;
- Bioelectric Overdrive actively pushes neuromuscular signaling beyond ordinary safe operating levels.

Hard guardrails:
- tissue remains the bottleneck;
- accumulated strain matters;
- output is temporary;
- no external electricity;
- no healing.

Result: **PASS.**

## 6. Spatial Anchor

Core distinction:
- Blink Step relocates self.
- Spatial Anchor resists displacement/relocation for a target/zone.

Open blocker:
The record is not reconstruction-grade until reference frames are defined.

Questions still requiring design authority:
- Is the anchor relative to planet surface, room, vehicle, platform, or selected frame?
- What if the reference itself accelerates?
- How are inertia and momentum resolved?
- What happens when two spatial effects contest?
- Can the anchor be moved after creation?

Result: **PARTIAL.**

## 7. Memory Echo

Core distinction:
It reads residual impressions, not minds and not objective recorded history.

Hard guardrails:
- fragmentary;
- biased;
- degradable;
- context-sensitive;
- no guaranteed identity;
- no memory alteration.

World consequence:
Courts/institutions should not automatically treat an Echo as infallible evidence.

Result: **PASS WITH GUARDRAIL.**

## 8. Momentum Bank

Resolved Common boundary:
- Impact Cushion dissipates impact into lower-risk forms.
- Momentum Bank stores a portion of kinetic energy for later release.

Hard guardrails:
- hard reserve ceiling;
- no static pressure storage;
- residual force remains;
- no net energy creation;
- release carries recoil/collateral risk.

Result: **PASS.**

## 9. Umbra Veil

Core distinction:
- Lumen Pulse generates visible light.
- Umbra Veil redirects visible light around a bounded subject/area.

Hard guardrails:
- visible-spectrum limitation unless explicitly evolved;
- nonvisual sensors remain valid;
- movement/observer-angle complexity matters;
- no perfect all-angle invisibility by default.

Result: **PASS.**

## 10. Crystal Resonance

The mechanical seed is coherent:
compatible crystalline structures can be sensed/tuned/dampened/excited.

However, rarity and strategic value cannot be calibrated without THE GAME's own crystal ontology.

Blocked questions:
- what crystals are;
- whether crystals are technological, biological, cosmic, mineral, or mixed;
- how common crystal devices are;
- whether crystals are central infrastructure;
- what energy/resonance rules exist;
- whether resonance can damage or reprogram devices.

Do not import assumptions from another project.

Result: **BLOCKED PENDING WORLD CANON.**

## Cross-tier audit decisions

Resolved:
- Static Reservoir ↔ Lightning Conduit.
- Impact Cushion ↔ Momentum Bank.
- Heat Shaping ↔ Cryo Sink.
- Blink Step ↔ Spatial Anchor conceptually, but spatial physics remain unresolved.
- Lumen Pulse ↔ Umbra Veil.

Remaining higher-tier comparisons:
- Gravity Well ↔ Mass Inversion.
- Phase Edge ↔ future higher spatial/matter effects.
- Cryo Sink ↔ Energy Devour if energy absorption overlaps.
- Bioelectric Overdrive ↔ higher time/biological amplification effects.
- Spatial Anchor ↔ Fold Step / World Gate / higher spatial control.
- Crystal Resonance ↔ final crystal system.

## Phase-C result

Rare 001–009 are coherent enough to remain in the calibration corpus.

Rare 010 remains a valid placeholder record but is **world-canon blocked**.

No Rare ability is canon-promoted by this audit.
