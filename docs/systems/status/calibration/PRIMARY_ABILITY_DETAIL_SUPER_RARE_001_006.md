## ABILITY_SRR_001 — Temporal Drag

Core law:
Reduces the rate of physical processes inside a small external zone relative to the user.

Boundaries:
- bounded zone only;
- no full time stop;
- no reversal/rewind;
- living targets resist deeper slowing;
- user is not automatically accelerated;
- radius, slowdown factor, duration, and stacking are `TBD`;
- causality outside the zone remains normal.

Rarity rationale:
Super Rare because it directly alters process-rate relationships rather than ordinary motion, while still being local, expensive, and incomplete.

Relevant attributes/skills:
Will, Intellect, Perception; Tactics, Investigation, Powers knowledge.

Resource model:
Focus + Resolve; cost rises sharply with slowdown depth, radius, duration, and living resistance.

Progression:
weak drag → precise zone → sustained drag → selective timing window → advanced high-cost slowdown.

Counters:
range, small radius, living resistance, concentration, resource drain, leaving the zone, time/law counter-abilities.

Failure/danger:
desynchronization, collisions at zone boundary, user confusion, delayed mechanical processes, dangerous interruption.

World roles:
containment, rescue, research, security, controlled combat applications.

Upper-tier guardrail:
Must not become time reversal, full stop, or broad self-acceleration.

## ABILITY_SRR_002 — Fold Step

Core law:
Briefly folds two visible stable points together so a person or object can cross the shortened path.

Boundaries:
- stable endpoints required;
- visible/known endpoint rules remain `TBD`;
- finite mass;
- finite opening duration;
- no permanent portal;
- no interplanetary/interworld travel by default;
- spatial interference can collapse the fold;
- transit orientation/momentum/reference-frame behavior remains a major blocker.

Rare adjacency:
`ABILITY_RAR_006 Spatial Anchor` resists spatial relocation. Fold Step actively creates a temporary shortened spatial connection.

Resource model:
Focus; mass, distance, duration, aperture size, and endpoint instability increase burden.

Progression:
small-object fold → self crossing → passenger/object transit → short sustained aperture → high-precision fold.

Counters:
Spatial Anchor, unstable endpoints, high mass, interference, line-of-sight denial if required, endpoint occupation.

Failure/danger:
partial transit, momentum mismatch, endpoint shift, collision, aperture collapse.

World roles:
rescue, transport, security, research, military logistics.

Upper-tier guardrail:
Must not become World Gate or permanent infrastructure-scale portal creation.

## ABILITY_SRR_003 — Adaptive Regeneration

Core law:
Accelerates the user's biological regeneration and becomes more efficient against injury patterns the body has previously survived.

Boundaries:
- primarily self-targeting unless later explicitly expanded;
- no resurrection;
- cannot restore lost identity/brain-state after death;
- missing material still matters;
- adaptation requires survival/history;
- energy, nutrients, biomass, and time remain real costs;
- complete limb/organ restoration is not assumed until explicitly calibrated.

Uncommon adjacency:
`ABILITY_UNC_010 Knit Flesh` accelerates natural healing on self/touched targets. Adaptive Regeneration is Super Rare because it can achieve much deeper self-repair and improve efficiency against previously survived injury patterns.

Resource model:
Stamina + focus + biological reserve.

Progression:
accelerated superficial repair → deep tissue regeneration → repeated-injury efficiency → major repair → advanced adaptive recovery.

Counters:
massive destruction, missing biomass, starvation/dehydration, toxins/infection if not separately countered, repeated novel injury, energy exhaustion.

Failure/danger:
metabolic collapse, malformed regeneration, hidden damage, nutrient depletion, adaptation overconfidence.

World roles:
medicine, military, research, high-risk exploration, long-term biological study.

Upper-tier guardrail:
Must not become instant resurrection, identity restoration, or arbitrary biological transformation.

## ABILITY_SRR_004 — Neural Override

Core law:
Precisely controls the user's own nervous signaling and can disrupt a touched target's peripheral neural transmission.

Boundaries:
- self-control can affect signal timing, sensory gating, pain response, motor output, and autonomic functions only within survivable limits;
- external effect requires contact;
- external effect targets peripheral neural transmission, not memories/personality;
- no remote mind control;
- no thought reading;
- duration/depth/nerve groups `TBD`.

Rare adjacency:
Bioelectric Overdrive boosts neuromuscular performance. Neural Override is broader and more precise: it can selectively gate/synchronize the user's nervous system and externally disrupt peripheral signaling through touch.

Resource model:
Focus + Resolve; extreme self-modulation may add physiological burden.

Progression:
self sensory gate → motor control → autonomic modulation → touched peripheral disruption → advanced selective override.

Counters:
distance, protective barriers, no contact, nerve damage/atypical biology, concentration, cognitive overload.

Failure/danger:
arrhythmia/autonomic instability, sensory blackout, paralysis, self-injury, target nerve injury.

World roles:
medicine, research, security, interrogation ethics, disability support, combat.

Upper-tier guardrail:
No memory rewrite, personality rewrite, remote domination, or omniscient neural control.

## ABILITY_SRR_005 — Mass Inversion

Core law:
Temporarily reverses the gravitational response of a tagged object within a bounded mass limit.

Boundaries:
- valid tag/mark/contact required;
- inertia is not reversed;
- only gravitational response changes;
- target mass ceiling `TBD`;
- duration `TBD`;
- no arbitrary gravity field for untagged matter;
- no negative inertial mass;
- no planet-scale effect.

Rare adjacency:
`ABILITY_RAR_001 Gravity Well` creates an inward gravity field affecting an area. Mass Inversion changes how one tagged object responds to gravity, enabling upward acceleration relative to the local field.

Resource model:
Focus; cost scales with mass, duration, and repeated tag control.

Progression:
small object inversion → precise timing → heavier object → multiple prepared tags if later approved → advanced controlled inversion.

Counters:
remove/deny tag, exceed mass limit, unstable environment, Spatial Anchor-style constraints, concentration pressure, enclosed spaces.

Failure/danger:
ceiling collisions, uncontrolled ascent, inertial collisions, dropped target on effect end, structural hazards.

World roles:
cargo handling, rescue, construction, transport, research, military.

Upper-tier guardrail:
No universal gravity reversal and no inertia cancellation.

## ABILITY_SRR_006 — Energy Devour

Core law:
Absorbs defined forms of non-biological energy and converts part of them into a personal energy reserve.

Boundaries:
- energy-type whitelist required;
- no biological life-force absorption by default;
- no universal absorption;
- conversion efficiency is below 100% unless explicitly calibrated otherwise;
- reserve has a hard capacity;
- incompatible energy can injure the user;
- release/use of reserve must follow separately defined legal output routes;
- cannot absorb abstract concepts, Status effects, or time/space by default.

Rare adjacency:
- Lightning Conduit routes electrical energy but does not generally convert it into a universal reserve.
- Cryo Sink stores thermal energy in a thermal-specific reserve.
- Energy Devour is Super Rare because it can absorb several explicitly defined non-biological energy types into a generalized personal reserve.

Resource model:
Focus + energy reserve.

Progression:
one energy type → controlled conversion → second compatible type → sustained absorption → advanced multi-source handling.

Counters:
unsupported energy types, reserve saturation, overwhelming power, biological/mechanical attacks, no available energy, conversion instability.

Failure/danger:
catastrophic discharge, reserve rupture, thermal/electrical/radiative injury depending source, conversion failure.

World roles:
energy infrastructure, disaster response, research, containment, military/security.

Upper-tier guardrail:
Must not become universal power nullification or concept absorption.
