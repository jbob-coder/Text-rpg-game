# THE GAME — Super Epic Counterplay Detail 001–003

Status: **CALIBRATION DRAFT / NOT CANON / NOT IMPLEMENTED**

Parents:
- `PRIMARY_ABILITY_DETAIL_SUPER_EPIC_001_003.md`
- `ABILITY_COUNTER_PROFILES_WAVE_001.md`

Purpose: ensure Super Epic rarity remains bounded by meaningful failure conditions rather than raw rarity immunity.

## COUNTER_SEP_001 — Time Partition

Natural limits:
- body speed remains ordinary;
- sensory input can still be incomplete or deceptive;
- sustained partition creates heavy cognitive and physical recovery burden.

Counterplay:
- force rapid physical execution where thought alone cannot solve the problem;
- overload attention with conflicting information;
- deny reliable sensory data;
- extend the situation until resource/recovery limits matter.

Environmental pressure:
chaotic sensory conditions, poor visibility, rapidly changing physical constraints, and tasks dominated by raw movement reduce its advantage.

Failure condition:
The design fails if Time Partition becomes actual universal time acceleration, perfect prediction, or consequence-free infinite thinking time.

## COUNTER_SEP_002 — Matter Recode

Natural limits:
- nonliving matter only;
- valid composition knowledge required;
- mass and energy remain bounded;
- complexity and scale increase burden.

Counterplay:
- use unfamiliar, contaminated, mixed, or poorly characterized materials;
- deny time or information needed to model the target;
- force scale/complexity beyond the learned model;
- exploit energy/resource limits.

Environmental pressure:
heterogeneous materials, rapidly changing structures, unknown compounds, and limited analysis time reduce reliability.

Failure condition:
The design fails if Matter Recode becomes unrestricted matter creation, living-body rewriting, element creation without source material, or knowledge-free fabrication.

## COUNTER_SEP_003 — Probability Tilt

Natural limits:
- only already plausible outcomes;
- no guaranteed result;
- large probability gaps resist meaningful bias;
- context must remain bounded.

Counterplay:
- create deterministic conditions;
- reduce the number of near-equivalent outcomes;
- widen the gap between desired and undesired results;
- force context complexity beyond stable control.

Environmental pressure:
strongly constrained systems, verified deterministic mechanisms, and extremely unlikely target outcomes reduce effect.

Failure condition:
The design fails if Probability Tilt guarantees outcomes, makes impossible events occur, rewrites resolved events, or acts as unrestricted luck.

# Acceptance gate

Counterplay remains valid only if the parent ability keeps its hard law boundary. Higher rarity never removes the need for counters.
