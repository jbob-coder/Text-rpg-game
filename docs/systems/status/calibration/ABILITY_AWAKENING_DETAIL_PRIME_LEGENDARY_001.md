## AWAKE_PRL_001 — Law Silence

First manifestation:
A very small nearby interaction unexpectedly fails to resolve according to its normal rule for a brief interval, only where the user has enough intuitive or learned understanding to form a valid target.

Immediate instability:
- the user may identify the interaction incompletely;
- field boundaries may be irregular;
- linked unsuppressed mechanisms can create unexpected results;
- Focus/Resolve collapse and system strain can occur immediately;
- first use may end almost as soon as it begins.

Hard boundary:
The awakening does not grant a wish, concept deletion, Status suppression, permanent law change, or global effect.

Knowledge rule:
Status may identify `Law Silence` and `Prime Legendary` rarity according to the awakening/visibility standards. That classification does not reveal:
- what laws the user can successfully target;
- the user's validated rule library;
- exact system strain;
- protected rules;
- future evolution.

Institutional consequence:
A verified Prime Legendary awakening should trigger extraordinary attention under future world doctrine, but no named procedure is canon until institutional documentation is authored.

Promotion blockers:
- targetable-law schema;
- first-use knowledge threshold;
- field behavior;
- safe observation/testing doctrine;
- public/classified disclosure rules.
