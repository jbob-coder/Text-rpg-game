## AWAKE_LEG_001 — Event Reversal

First manifestation:
A very small nearby zone unexpectedly attempts to return toward a physical state from moments earlier.

Instability:
- the user may not understand what was recorded;
- zone boundaries may be poorly defined;
- excluded anchors may be absent or accidental;
- severe focus/resolve/system strain can follow.

Knowledge rule:
Status may identify Event Reversal and Legendary rarity, but first manifestation does not prove resurrection, memory rewriting, or unlimited timeline control.

Hard boundary:
Only a bounded recent physical-state restoration is expressed.

## AWAKE_LEG_002 — World Gate

First manifestation:
A brief unstable spatial connection attempts to form only if a compatible calibrated anchor relationship and sufficient energy are already present.

Instability:
- failed endpoint lock;
- aperture instability;
- energy collapse;
- reference mismatch;
- unsafe environmental transfer.

Knowledge rule:
Status may identify World Gate and Legendary rarity, but first manifestation does not imply free unanchored teleportation.

Hard boundary:
No valid anchors and energy means no valid long-distance gate.
