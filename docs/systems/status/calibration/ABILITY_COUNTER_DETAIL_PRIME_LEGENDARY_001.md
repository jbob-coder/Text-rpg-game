## COUNTER_PRL_001 — Law Silence

Natural limits:
- requires precise understanding;
- targets one rule at a time by default;
- operates only inside a small field;
- duration is short;
- cost and system strain are severe;
- undefined/protected interactions cannot simply be named into suppression.

Counterplay:
- change conditions so the selected rule no longer matches;
- use parallel mechanisms governed by different rules;
- act outside the field;
- break concentration;
- force repeated activations;
- exploit incomplete knowledge;
- use protected/higher-order rules if later defined;
- pressure during post-use strain/recovery.

Environmental/system pressure:
Complex systems with many coupled interactions are harder to suppress cleanly because silencing one modeled rule may leave several other mechanisms active.

Information counterplay:
Concealing, changing, or misrepresenting mechanism details can matter only if the world rules establish that the user genuinely lacks sufficient understanding. This cannot become arbitrary GM denial.

Failure condition:
The design fails if Law Silence:
- suppresses vague concepts;
- ignores knowledge;
- affects unlimited area/duration;
- deletes several unrelated rules freely;
- permanently changes law;
- disables Status by default;
- has no meaningful strain or recovery.

# Acceptance gate

Prime Legendary counterplay must be systemic and knowledge-based, not “only another Prime Legendary can resist it.”
