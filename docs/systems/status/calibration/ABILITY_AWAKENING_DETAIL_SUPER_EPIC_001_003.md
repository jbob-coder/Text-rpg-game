## AWAKE_SEP_001 — Time Partition

First manifestation:
The user's subjective processing suddenly expands while external events continue at their normal physical rate.

Observable presentation:
- unusually long subjective decision time can occur inside a very short external interval;
- the body still moves at ordinary mechanically allowed speed;
- visible effects may be subtle or absent.

Immediate instability:
- sensory overload;
- planning faster than the body can execute;
- transition confusion when the partition ends;
- rapid focus, resolve, and stamina burden.

Knowledge rule:
Observers may identify the ability and rarity when Status access permits, but cannot infer actual acceleration factor or future decisions.

Hard boundary:
Awakening never grants external time stop, rewind, foresight, or automatic body-speed increase.

## AWAKE_SEP_002 — Matter Recode

First manifestation:
A small quantity of familiar nonliving matter partially rearranges toward a structure the user understands.

Observable presentation:
- shape, texture, purity, or internal arrangement may change;
- source mass remains bounded by the parent law;
- incomplete knowledge can produce an incomplete or unstable result.

Immediate instability:
- composition mismatch;
- incomplete conversion;
- unexpected energy burden;
- unstable product structure.

Knowledge rule:
Classification can identify Matter Recode and Super Epic rarity, but the event does not prove unrestricted fabrication or matter creation.

Hard boundary:
No living-body recode, element creation from nothing, or unknown-material generation.

## AWAKE_SEP_003 — Probability Tilt

First manifestation:
Inside a tightly bounded situation with several already plausible outcomes, one outcome family becomes modestly more likely.

Observable presentation:
- the effect may have no unique visual signature;
- repeated observation is needed to distinguish ability influence from coincidence;
- the result remains uncertain.

Immediate instability:
- unintended plausible outcomes may also shift;
- context may be defined too broadly;
- cognitive strain can rise without an obvious effect.

Knowledge rule:
Status may identify Probability Tilt and Super Epic rarity, but observers should not treat any single outcome as proof of deterministic control.

Hard boundary:
Impossible outcomes remain impossible; no guarantee, retroactive rewrite, or miracle effect is granted.
