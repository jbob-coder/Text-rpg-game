# THE GAME — Passive Detail Wave 001: Mental / Will 0001–0010

Status: **CALIBRATION DRAFT / NOT CANON / NOT IMPLEMENTED**

Purpose: deepen the ten existing Mental / Will passive records while preserving their current IDs, names, hidden-until-qualified policy, and recovery-based acquisition framing.

## PASSIVE_WIL_0001 — Stress Lock
Reduces task degradation during familiar acute stress. Does not remove the stress state or later recovery requirements.

## PASSIVE_WIL_0002 — Fear Processing
Improves return of decision quality after a fear response is recognized and processed. Does not prevent the initial response.

## PASSIVE_WIL_0003 — Pain Compartmentalization
Reduces distraction from stabilized pain while preserving authoritative injury and condition penalties.

## PASSIVE_WIL_0004 — Focus Under Fire
Improves concentration on a trained task during external threat. Direct interruption remains authoritative.

## PASSIVE_WIL_0005 — Interruption Resistance
Reduces task-context loss from minor interruptions on familiar resumable work.

## PASSIVE_WIL_0006 — Patience Engine
Improves stability during long deliberate waiting, observation, or repetitive precision tasks without removing fatigue or sleep requirements.

## PASSIVE_WIL_0007 — Crisis Clarity
Improves prioritization among already understood options during time-limited emergencies. Does not create missing expertise or information.

## PASSIVE_WIL_0008 — Resolve Reserve
Reduces Resolve drain once per defined sustained-pressure window. It is bounded reduction, not unlimited regeneration.

## PASSIVE_WIL_0009 — Emotional Recovery
Improves return toward normal functional baseline after an intense but resolved emotional event without altering memory or personality.

## PASSIVE_WIL_0010 — Cognitive Endurance
Slows Focus degradation during prolonged reasoning or vigilance without raising Intellect or learning speed.

## Family guardrails
- Level does not directly scale these passives by default.
- Qualification remains hidden until allowed by Status visibility rules.
- Meaningful legitimate events and recovery matter; trivial farming does not.
- Emotion, stress, pain, injury, Focus, and Resolve remain separate authoritative states.
- Duplicate ownership does not stack.
- Exact numeric scaling remains TBD.

## Promotion blockers
Exact state ownership, numeric scaling, stacking, knowledge review, world institutions, save/projection behavior, and tests remain open.
