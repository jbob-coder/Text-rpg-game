## ABILITY_SEP_001 — Time Partition

Core law:
Accelerates the user's perception, planning, and neural processing relative to surrounding events.

Boundaries:
- the user's body does not automatically move faster;
- muscles, joints, inertia, and external mechanics remain normal;
- perception/decision acceleration creates a gap between thought-speed and action-speed;
- duration, subjective acceleration factor, sensory bandwidth, and recovery cost remain `TBD`;
- no external time stop;
- no rewind;
- no automatic perfect prediction.

Epic boundary:
Temporal Drag changes process rate in an external zone. Time Partition changes the user's subjective processing rate without granting matching body acceleration.

Why Super Epic:
It creates a persistent cognition/action-time mismatch that can transform planning, targeting, analysis, and reaction selection even while external mechanics stay ordinary.

Resources:
Focus + Resolve + Stamina.

Progression:
brief subjective expansion → multi-step planning → sensory integration → extended partition → advanced high-load cognition window.

Counters:
sensory overload, deceptive information, body-speed limits, exhaustion, tasks requiring raw movement rather than decision quality.

Failure modes:
over-planning, delayed body response, confusion when exiting the state, severe cognitive/recovery burden.

World roles:
command, research, high-speed analysis, advanced piloting, strategic operations.

Upper-tier guardrail:
Must not become actual global time acceleration or perfect foresight.

## ABILITY_SEP_002 — Matter Recode

Core law:
Rearranges nonliving matter at molecular scale within strict mass, composition-knowledge, and energy limits.

Boundaries:
- nonliving matter only;
- composition must be understood to a defined threshold;
- total mass is conserved unless future canon explicitly defines otherwise;
- energy cost follows the required structural/chemical change;
- cannot safely alter living organisms;
- cannot create elements from nothing;
- cannot produce unknown materials without a valid learned model;
- scale, complexity, precision, and energy reserve remain `TBD`.

Epic boundary:
Adaptive Arsenal transforms the user's own biological tissues using learned forms. Matter Recode transforms external nonliving matter based on structural knowledge.

Why Super Epic:
It changes material configuration at a fundamental scale and can convert one valid arrangement into another without conventional fabrication steps.

Resources:
Focus + energy reserve.

Progression:
simple molecular rearrangement → purified/restructured material → composite transformation → complex manufactured structure → advanced multi-material recode.

Counters:
unknown composition, mixed/contaminated material, insufficient energy, scale limits, interruption, materials outside learned models.

Failure modes:
incorrect composition, unstable product, incomplete conversion, energy loss, structural weakness.

World roles:
manufacturing, repair, construction, research, logistics, recycling, strategic industry.

Upper-tier guardrail:
No living-body recode, element creation without source material, or unlimited matter creation.

## ABILITY_SEP_003 — Probability Tilt

Core law:
Biases likelihood among already plausible near-equivalent outcomes inside a bounded context.

Boundaries:
- impossible outcomes remain impossible;
- cannot guarantee one chosen result;
- stronger bias requires closer competing probabilities;
- context must be bounded;
- cannot directly create matter/energy;
- no retroactive rewriting of resolved events;
- exact probability shift, context size, duration, and observability remain `TBD`.

Epic boundary:
Causal Mark restores a recorded property. Probability Tilt acts before resolution by altering the weighting among plausible outcomes.

Why Super Epic:
It changes event-resolution likelihood rather than force, matter, space, or time directly.

Resources:
Focus + Resolve.

Progression:
minor tie-break bias → selected outcome family → sustained bounded context → multiple related checks → advanced near-equivalent probability shaping.

Counters:
highly deterministic situations, impossible/near-impossible desired outcomes, large probability gaps, chaotic contexts with too many uncontrolled variables, concentration pressure.

Failure modes:
wrong outcome family, weak/no observable effect, unintended nearby plausible outcome becoming more likely, heavy cognitive/resource debt.

World roles:
research, strategy, navigation, negotiations, high-stakes operations, statistical analysis.

Upper-tier guardrail:
No guaranteed outcomes, miracle creation, retroactive editing, or “luck solves everything.”

# Super Epic acceptance principle

Super Epic abilities modify a foundational resolution dimension:
- subjective processing rate;
- molecular material arrangement;
- probability weighting.

They must remain bounded enough that the world can still produce uncertainty, failure, and meaningful constraints.

No canon promotion is implied.
