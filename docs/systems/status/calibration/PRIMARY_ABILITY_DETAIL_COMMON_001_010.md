## ABILITY_COM_001 — Kinetic Palm

### Identity
- display name: **Kinetic Palm**
- aliases: `[]`
- rarity: **Common**
- family: `kinetic`
- stable version: `0.1-calibration`
- design status: `CALIBRATION_PROPOSAL`
- canon status: `NOT_CANON_UNTIL_PROMOTED`
- implementation status: `NOT_IMPLEMENTED`

### Core law
The user applies a short directional impulse from one or both hands to a touched or near-contact target. The ability changes momentum over a brief impulse window; it does not provide continuous telekinesis.

### Capability boundaries
- allowed: brief push, pull, deflection, recoil assistance, controlled knockback, object redirection at contact range;
- forbidden: sustained levitation, remote free-form telekinesis, continuous acceleration, internal-organ targeting through intact matter;
- range: touch to near-contact; exact maximum `TBD`;
- target: user-contacted objects/creatures within mass/control limits;
- LOS: normally yes for near-contact use;
- contact: direct or near-contact hand projection;
- area: single target by default; broad two-hand pulse remains an advanced application only if later calibrated;
- duration: impulse-scale;
- persistence: momentum persists physically after the effect ends;
- stacking: repeated impulses allowed but resource and stability costs accumulate;
- environmental constraint: effectiveness depends on target mass, anchoring, friction, and available reaction force.

### Awakening
At age 18 the first manifestation should be a short involuntary or semi-controlled shove from the hands. The event must not introduce unrelated telekinetic behavior. Primary hazards are accidental knockback, dropped objects, and destabilizing nearby people.

### Rarity rationale
Common because the law is narrow, legible, historically teachable, and expected to have established counter/training doctrine. High skill can still make it dangerous.

### Level interaction
- raw output: `TBD` pending Level-reward canon;
- range growth: not assumed;
- technique capacity: may expand through mastery rather than Level alone;
- Level gates: none authored;
- Level 100: replacement opportunity handled only by the Level-100 standard.

### Attribute interactions
- Might: improves physical follow-through and bracing, not the ability's law by itself;
- Agility: improves timing and directional placement;
- Endurance: supports repeated use and recoil management;
- Intellect: supports force estimation and safe applications;
- Will: supports control under stress;
- Perception: improves target timing and trajectory judgment;
- Presence: no direct mechanical interaction authored.

### Skill interactions
Relevant candidates: Athletics, Tactics, Powers knowledge, close-quarters training, rescue handling. Skill links remain content-level until the skill taxonomy is finalized.

### Resource model
- primary cost: stamina + focus;
- exact cost: `TBD`;
- cooldown: no hard cooldown assumed; repeated use creates fatigue and control degradation;
- long-term strain: wrist/shoulder/elbow loading and cognitive fatigue if overused.

### Mastery structure
1. discovery: basic one-hand impulse;
2. control: consistent direction and force;
3. precision: controlled object/limb deflection;
4. efficiency: lower waste/recoil;
5. advanced: chained or shaped impulses without becoming sustained telekinesis.

### Technique map
- `TECH_COM_001_T1` — **Palm Push**: safe single-direction contact impulse;
- `TECH_COM_001_T2` — **Vector Tap**: low-output precision deflection;
- `TECH_COM_001_T3` — **Brace Drive**: repeated controlled impulses while braced;
- `TECH_COM_001_T4` — **Twin-Hand Burst**: higher-output two-hand impulse with larger recoil/recovery window.

### Evolution
No evolution route is canon. Legal future routes must preserve the impulse law rather than becoming general telekinesis.

### Passive synergies
Potential links: grip/endurance, guard recovery, precision scaling, cast stability. Exact stable passive links remain review items.

### Counters / weaknesses
Heavy anchoring, distance denial, unstable footing, forced repeated use, concentration disruption, and environments where collateral objects make impulse use unsafe.

### Failure / danger
Mis-aimed impulse, self-stagger from poor bracing, joint strain, unintended knockback, collateral property damage. Catastrophic behavior requires an explicit later threshold and may not be inferred from rarity.

### World knowledge / law
Publicly understandable in broad form. Exact institutional policy is `TBD`; dangerous-use law should treat unwanted kinetic force as ordinary assault/property-damage capable conduct rather than granting legal immunity because it is an ability.

### Known users
`TBD`. No historical or current user is invented here.

### Visual / content requirements
Palm-origin distortion/impact FX, compact Status icon, training targets, rescue/noncombat examples, overuse feedback, public-awakening reaction content, and at least one environment where careless use is tactically bad.

## ABILITY_COM_002 — Thermal Sight

### Identity
- rarity: **Common**
- family: `sensory`
- stable version: `0.1-calibration`
- statuses: `CALIBRATION_PROPOSAL / NOT_CANON_UNTIL_PROMOTED / NOT_IMPLEMENTED`

### Core law
The user perceives temperature gradients and heat signatures beyond ordinary visible-light vision.

### Capability boundaries
- allowed: relative heat mapping, warm-body detection, recent thermal traces where physically plausible, equipment hot-spot detection;
- forbidden: creating heat, seeing through every material, perfect identification of individuals, measuring exact temperature without calibration;
- range: `TBD`, limited by contrast, atmosphere, obstruction, and user control;
- target: emitted/transferred thermal patterns, not minds or hidden metadata;
- LOS: requires a physically meaningful thermal path;
- persistence: none; perception only;
- major environmental counters: insulation, thermal saturation, heat clutter, reflective/complex surfaces, extreme weather.

### Awakening
Initial manifestation is sudden overlay or replacement of ordinary visual interpretation with thermal contrast. Hazards are disorientation, overload, false interpretation, and impaired ordinary vision during the event.

### Rarity rationale
Common because sensory augmentation is narrow, trainable, non-generative, and likely institutionally well understood.

### Level / attributes / skills
No automatic Level range increase is assumed. Perception is the strongest direct attribute relationship; Intellect supports interpretation; Will supports overload control. Relevant skills include tracking, medicine, technical diagnostics, investigation, and Powers knowledge.

### Resource model
Focus cost while active; exact drain `TBD`. Extended use causes eye strain/headache/cognitive fatigue rather than physical heat production.

### Mastery structure
Detection → contrast control → source discrimination → rapid mode switching → multi-source interpretation.

### Technique map
- `TECH_COM_002_T1` — **Heat Overlay**
- `TECH_COM_002_T2` — **Contrast Focus**
- `TECH_COM_002_T3` — **Thermal Track**
- `TECH_COM_002_T4` — **Diagnostic Sweep**

### Evolution
Must remain thermal perception/interpretation unless an explicit evolution transforms the core law.

### Counters / failure
Insulation, decoys, background saturation, fires, heat-reflective surfaces, overlapping sources. Failure modes include misclassification and sensory overload.

### World integration
Likely useful in rescue, maintenance, medicine, security, hunting, and firefighting. Privacy law and surveillance doctrine are `TBD`; the ability does not justify seeing through all walls.

### Known users
