# THE GAME — Social / Behavioral Passive Effect Map 0001–0010

Status: **PHASE-C CALIBRATION / NOT CANON / NOT IMPLEMENTED**

Purpose: deepen the existing `PASSIVE_SOC_0001`–`PASSIVE_SOC_0010` records without changing their IDs or compact-wave identity.

| ID | Existing name | Bounded design effect |
|---|---|---|
| PASSIVE_SOC_0001 | Calm Presence | Reduces visible composure loss during familiar social pressure without removing the underlying emotion or stress. |
| PASSIVE_SOC_0002 | Lie Pattern Memory | Improves recall of previously observed contradiction patterns without converting contradiction into automatic deception detection. |
| PASSIVE_SOC_0003 | Audience Reading | Improves recognition of broad group mood from observable reactions while preserving uncertainty and individual variation. |
| PASSIVE_SOC_0004 | Negotiation Stamina | Reduces avoidable Focus/Resolve degradation during prolonged structured negotiation without granting persuasive authority. |
| PASSIVE_SOC_0005 | Social Recovery | Improves return toward normal functional baseline after demanding social encounters. |
| PASSIVE_SOC_0006 | Controlled Intimidation | Improves control over intensity and presentation when deliberately applying social pressure; it does not guarantee compliance. |
| PASSIVE_SOC_0007 | Rapport Habit | Improves consistency of behaviors that preserve already-established rapport. |
| PASSIVE_SOC_0008 | Boundary Sense | Improves recognition of explicit or strongly signaled interpersonal boundaries; ambiguity remains ambiguous. |
| PASSIVE_SOC_0009 | Conflict De-escalation | Improves execution of practiced de-escalation methods when meaningful dialogue remains possible. |
| PASSIVE_SOC_0010 | Reputation Awareness | Improves recall of already-known reputation consequences before public social choices. |

## Family guardrails

- These passives do not alter another character's agency.
- They do not create hidden social facts.
- Persuasion, deception, intimidation, empathy, leadership, reputation, relationships, and world knowledge remain separate systems.
- Observable reactions are not guaranteed truth.
- Social pressure does not become mind control.
- Same-resolver effects require capped composition.
- Duplicate ownership never stacks.
- Exact numeric values remain `TBD`.

## Promotion blockers

Social-context taxonomy, observable-reaction state, relationship/reputation boundaries, recovery ownership, cross-family stacking, knowledge review, numeric calibration, world norms/institutions, save/projection behavior, and tests remain open.
