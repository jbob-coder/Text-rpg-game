# THE GAME — Level 100 Exception Standard

Status: **ACTIVE TARGET-GAME DESIGN / HISTORICAL DETAILS OPEN**

Purpose: define the design boundary around the extraordinary Level-100 exception without inventing the two historical users or the final replacement ritual/mechanism before those details are authored.

---

# 1. Locked canon

- Level 100 exists.
- It is extraordinarily rare.
- Only **two humans in known history** have reached Level 100 so far.
- Reaching Level 100 creates the exceptional possibility of changing or replacing the otherwise fixed primary awakened ability.

These facts are owner-established canon.

---

# 2. What Level 100 does not automatically mean

Level 100 does not automatically mean:
- omnipotence;
- invulnerability;
- maximum value in every attribute;
- mastery of every skill;
- ownership of every passive;
- maximum profession/faction/institutional rank;
- automatic victory against lower-Level opponents;
- automatic ability replacement.

Level remains one axis inside the larger character system.

---

# 3. Replacement opportunity

The Level-100 rule should be understood as an **exception opportunity**, not a free menu command.

The final design must define:
- whether the original ability is lost, transformed, sealed, or retained in any form;
- whether replacement is chosen or discovered;
- whether the replacement can be any rarity;
- whether Unique abilities obey special restrictions;
- whether the cosmic system offers candidates;
- whether a ritual/event/location is required;
- resource/cost/risk;
- permanence;
- failure;
- whether the choice can ever be repeated.

No implementation should invent these details before the child design is completed.

---

# 4. Historical significance

The two Level-100 humans must eventually receive reconstruction-grade historical records covering:
- identity;
- birth/death/current status;
- original ability;
- rarity;
- primary techniques;
- Level-100 date;
- circumstances;
- whether they exercised the replacement exception;
- public account;
- classified account;
- war/political impact;
- surviving evidence;
- current cultural interpretation.

Until those records exist, names and details are intentionally open.

---

# 5. Knowledge asymmetry

The public may know that only two humans reached Level 100 while not knowing:
- the exact mechanism;
- whether both changed abilities;
- whether records were suppressed;
- what the cosmic system displayed;
- whether governments possess more complete evidence.

A future history/Status visibility packet must separate public fact from classified truth.

---

# 6. Ability replacement constraints

The final rule should preserve the importance of the one-primary-ability system.

Therefore replacement should not become an ordinary respec mechanic.

Design guardrails:
- Level 100 alone should not trivialize identity;
- replacement should create irreversible or near-irreversible consequences;
- previous training/history should still matter;
- techniques tied specifically to the old ability may become invalid, transformed, archived, or lost according to final rules;
- passives must be reviewed individually for compatibility;
- social/legal records may still preserve the person's original awakening history.

---

# 7. Unique interaction

Unique abilities require special handling.

Open questions:
- can a Level-100 user replace away from a Unique ability?
- if so, what happens to the one-per-universe slot?
- can another human later awaken it?
- does the ability cease to exist?
- can a Level-100 user replace into a Unique ability?
- can the cosmic system create a new Unique identity?

No default answer is implied.

---

# 8. Story function

Level 100 should operate as:
- historical mystery;
- extreme long-range aspiration;
- worldbuilding proof of the system's upper boundary;
- political/military/cosmic concern;
- source of myths, classified research, false claims, cults, and strategic planning.

It should not dominate ordinary early-game pacing.

---

# 9. Implementation gate

Do not implement the replacement mechanic until:
- Level/XP curve is calibrated;
- ability registry schema is stable;
- save implications are defined;
- passive compatibility is defined;
- two historical Level-100 records are authored;
- UI/confirmation/safety behavior is documented;
- tests cover irreversible state transitions.

---

# 10. Reconstruction acceptance

A future implementation packet must answer:
- what exactly happens at Level 100;
- what choice exists;
- what can/cannot be replaced;
- what survives replacement;
- how Unique abilities behave;
- how public/classified history records the event;
- why only two humans reached it so far.
