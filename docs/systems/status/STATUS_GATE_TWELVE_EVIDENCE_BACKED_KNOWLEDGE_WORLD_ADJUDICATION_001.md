## 1. Adjudication states

- `LOCAL_CONTEXT_SUPPORTED` — confirmed Gate Twelve functions provide a believable place/context in which the mechanic could matter.
- `PROFESSIONAL_CHANNEL_PLAUSIBLE` — local work/service evidence supports a professional knowledge channel in principle, but no training provider or knowledge owner is yet established.
- `INSTITUTION_REQUIRED` — local context exists, but the mechanic cannot advance to entity-linked world integration until an actual institution/role/protocol is authored.
- `NO_EVIDENCE_FOR_CLASSIFICATION` — current local evidence does not support treating the mechanic/method as classified.
- `NO_LOCAL_TRAINING_EVIDENCE` — the area supports use context but not proof that the passive is taught/unlocked there.
- `PARENT_WORLD_DECISION_BLOCKED` — progression to named world entity depends on unresolved parent-world canon.
