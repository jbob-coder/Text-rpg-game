## Family guardrails

- Adaptation reduces performance loss; it does not erase environmental danger.
- Heat/cold/altitude/sleep/nutrition states remain authoritative.
- Environmental knowledge does not equal perfect prediction.
- No passive grants universal resistance to unknown hazards.
- Same-resolver effects require capped composition.
- Duplicate ownership never stacks.
- Exact numeric values remain `TBD`.

## Promotion blockers

Environment-state taxonomy, acclimation windows, recovery/reset rules, travel/task ownership, cross-family stacking, knowledge review, numeric calibration, world training doctrine, save/projection behavior, and tests remain open.
