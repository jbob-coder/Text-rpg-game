# THE GAME — Status Ability/Passive World Integration Standard

Status: **TARGET-DESIGN SCHEMA / WORLD POPULATION DEFERRED / NOT IMPLEMENTED**

Purpose: define the world fields required before an ability or passive is socially integrated into THE GAME.

## Ability integration fields
Each mature ability should eventually document public knowledge, school knowledge, government knowledge, research knowledge, faction knowledge, awakening disclosure, registration/classification handling, training access, profession value, economic value, strategic value, legal restrictions, controlled-use requirements, public attention, myths, historical cases, regional variation, provenance, and status.

## Passive integration fields
Each mature passive should document prevalence, method knowledge, schools/training sources, profession-specific knowledge, institutional knowledge, false methods, professional value, significant historical examples, regional variation, provenance, and status.

Knowledge of an unlock method does not reveal hidden Status progress.

## Institutional role classes
Until named organizations exist, use role classes such as `SCHOOL_AUTHORITY`, `CIVIL_GOVERNMENT`, `ABILITY_REGULATOR`, `MILITARY_SECURITY`, `RESEARCH_INSTITUTION`, `HEALTH_SERVICE`, `TRANSPORT_AUTHORITY`, `INDUSTRIAL_BODY`, `PROFESSIONAL_ASSOCIATION`, `FACTION`, and `MEDIA_PUBLIC`.

Later world records map role classes to actual organizations by region and period.

## Rarity versus policy
Rarity can influence attention but does not directly determine policy. Policy also depends on capability, controllability, infrastructure value, known limitations, user history, and political context.

## Training access
Document instructor availability, facilities, equipment, time/cost, geographic availability, formal versus self-directed routes, and any restricted doctrine.

## Profession/economic integration
Document where a mechanic changes productivity, access requirements, compensation, licensing, liability, or scarcity value. World relevance is not limited to combat.

## Awakening integration
For age-18 awakening eventually define what the student sees, what school officials record, what becomes public, what remains private, and what follow-up procedure is triggered.

## Historical case record
Significant cases should use stable IDs and record date/period, location, mechanic IDs, involved entities, public account, private/classified account where applicable, verified facts, disputed claims, consequences, surviving evidence, and current interpretation.

## Regional variation
Knowledge and policy may vary by nation, settlement, school, faction, profession, and historical period. Do not assume one global policy.

## Integration status
Recommended states: `UNMAPPED`, `ROLE_CLASSES_MAPPED`, `WORLD_ENTITIES_LINKED`, `HISTORY_LINKED`, `LAW_POLICY_LINKED`, `CANON_REVIEWED`, `IMPLEMENTATION_MAPPED`.

Mechanic canon status and world-integration status remain separate.

## Initial priority
Prioritize rare+ abilities, spatial/transport mechanics, biological mechanics, energy/infrastructure mechanics, evidence/investigation mechanics, Unique/Prime Legendary abilities, and passives whose current knowledge profiles appear implausibly restricted.

No Wave-001 mechanic is canon-promoted by this standard.
