| ID | Existing name | Bounded design effect |
|---|---|---|
| PASSIVE_INJ_0001 | Scar Flexibility | Reduces mobility loss associated with fully healed scar tissue after proper rehabilitation. |
| PASSIVE_INJ_0002 | Compensated Gait | Improves movement efficiency around a medically stable long-term gait change. |
| PASSIVE_INJ_0003 | One-Hand Adaptation | Improves practiced task performance using one available hand after legitimate adaptation. |

| PASSIVE_INJ_0005 | Joint Protection | Improves protective movement habits around a previously injured stabilized joint. |
| PASSIVE_INJ_0006 | Vision Compensation | Improves use of remaining visual cues after a persistent documented visual limitation. |
| PASSIVE_INJ_0007 | Hearing Compensation | Improves use of remaining auditory/visual cues after a persistent documented hearing limitation. |
| PASSIVE_INJ_0008 | Breath Compensation | Improves pacing/breathing habits around a medically stable respiratory limitation. |
| PASSIVE_INJ_0009 | Old Wound Warning | Improves recognition of early familiar warning sensations from a documented old injury. |
| PASSIVE_INJ_0010 | Rehabilitation Strength | Improves strength retention/rebuilding efficiency inside an authorized rehabilitation program. |

Guardrails:
- these passives adapt to documented stable limitations; they do not erase injury history;
- rehabilitation and medical stability remain prerequisites;
- no reinjury-farming route;
- no hidden diagnosis is created;
- exact values remain `TBD`.

Promotion blockers: injury-state taxonomy, rehabilitation authorization/state, compensation mechanics, cross-family stacking, knowledge review, save/projection mapping, and tests.
