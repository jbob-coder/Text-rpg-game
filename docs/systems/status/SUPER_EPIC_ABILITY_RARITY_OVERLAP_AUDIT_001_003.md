## Audit rule

Super Epic must represent a qualitative change in what dimension of reality/system resolution the user can influence. It must not merely multiply Epic output.

## ABILITY_SEP_001 — Time Partition

Result: **PASS WITH MODEL BLOCKER**

Distinct law:
- changes subjective cognition/perception rate;
- does not change external process rate;
- does not automatically accelerate the body.

Nearest lower-tier relationship:
- `ABILITY_SRR_001 Temporal Drag` changes physical process rate inside an external zone;
- Time Partition changes the user's internal subjective processing relationship to unchanged external events.

Why the boundary holds:
The two abilities operate on different targets and different rules. One affects external process rate; the other affects cognition.

Blockers:
- subjective-time multiplier semantics;
- sensory bandwidth;
- metabolic/recovery accounting;
- interaction with initiative/reaction systems.

## ABILITY_SEP_002 — Matter Recode

Result: **PASS WITH WORLD/PHYSICS BLOCKER**

Distinct law:
- rearranges external nonliving matter at molecular scale;
- requires composition knowledge and bounded energy/mass.

Nearest lower-tier relationship:
- `ABILITY_EPI_003 Adaptive Arsenal` transforms the user's own biological tissue using learned biological forms;
- Matter Recode affects external nonliving matter and uses structural/composition knowledge.

Why the boundary holds:
Self-biological transformation and external nonliving molecular rearrangement remain separate domains.

Blockers:
- mass conservation implementation;
- energy accounting;
- composition-knowledge threshold;
- material complexity model;
- world industrial/legal consequences.

## ABILITY_SEP_003 — Probability Tilt

Result: **PASS WITH RESOLUTION-SEMANTICS BLOCKER**

Distinct law:
- biases weighting among already plausible unresolved outcomes;
- cannot guarantee a result;
- cannot make an impossible result possible;
- cannot rewrite resolved history.

Nearest lower-tier relationship:
- `ABILITY_EPI_001 Causal Mark` records one bounded physical property and can restore that property within a limited window;
- Probability Tilt acts before resolution and alters likelihood rather than restoring a recorded property.

Why the boundary holds:
Probability Tilt is prospective and uncertain. Causal Mark is restorative and property-specific.

Blockers:
- definition of a “bounded context”;
- probability shift ceiling;
- handling of hidden/unknown probabilities;
- observability and auditability;
- anti-save-scumming / repeated-resolution rules.

## Upper-tier guardrails

Super Epic mastery must not silently become:
- global time stop/rewind;
- unrestricted living-body molecular rewriting;
- matter creation from nothing;
- guaranteed fate or impossible-event generation.

Those changes would require a separate ability law and rarity review.
