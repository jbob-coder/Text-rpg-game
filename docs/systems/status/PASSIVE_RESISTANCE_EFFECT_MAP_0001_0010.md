| PASSIVE_RES_0002 | Cold Resistance | Reduces bounded cold-related harm/performance loss after safe adaptation; never immunity. |

| PASSIVE_RES_0008 | Fear Resistance | Reduces behavioral disruption from familiar fear stimuli while preserving threat awareness. |
| PASSIVE_RES_0009 | Fatigue Resistance | Reduces performance loss from accumulated ordinary fatigue; recovery remains required. |
| PASSIVE_RES_0010 | Disorientation Resistance | Improves reorientation after familiar non-injury motion/disorientation events. |
