## 1. Knowledge rule

The world may understand an adaptation pattern without knowing:
- the Status passive's exact stable identity;
- its exact internal qualification threshold;
- whether a particular person is close to qualification;
- the cosmic/system mechanism deciding reveal.

Therefore this packet separates:
- broad training knowledge;
- confirmed passive existence;
- exact unlock method;
- classified optimization data.

No player-facing projection inherits these institutional facts automatically.

## 3. PASSIVE_PHY_0001 — Iron Tendons

### Proposed knowledge state
- public existence: known;
- public exact method: unknown;
- school knowledge: known broad conditioning principles;
- government: known;
- military/security: known and used in conditioning doctrine where relevant;
- research: confirmed/studied;
- faction knowledge: mixed by training sophistication;
- classification: none by default;
- player knowledge: event/education dependent.

### Public theory
Progressive high-load training with recovery can produce unusually durable tendon adaptation.

### Exact-method rule
The public may know the broad method without knowing the Status threshold represented by `UNLOCK_PHY_0001`.

### False rumors
Possible low-grade rumor: "maximum-weight lifting is the fastest route." This is incomplete/dangerous because qualification also depends on legitimate repeated sessions and recovery rather than isolated overload.

### Historical-case requirement
Future world integration should add:
- at least one ordinary profession/training case;
- at least one failed overtraining case;
- no invented named person until character history is authored.

Recommendation: **RETAIN current broad-knowledge posture.**

## 13. Military/security knowledge standard for mundane adaptation

For ordinary physical adaptation passives, military/security organizations may know:
- existence;
- broad training method;
- risk profile;
- selection/conditioning relevance;
- approximate prevalence from internal records.

They should not automatically know:
- exact cosmic-system threshold;
- every person's hidden progress;
- all future evolutions;
- hidden system mechanics.

## 14. Research knowledge standard

Research institutions may have:
- cohort observations;
- correlation between training history and reveal;
- medical/physiological measurements;
- competing threshold models;
- historical cases.

Research knowledge is empirical and can remain incomplete even when the engine has an exact hidden rule.

This preserves a world where scientists can be very informed without becoming omniscient readers of game logic.

## 15. False-rumor provenance classes

Future records should tag rumors as:
- folk simplification;
- commercial training claim;
- institutional misinformation;
- deliberate faction deception;
- historical misinterpretation;
- outdated research;
- malicious exploit bait.

The current Physical slice especially benefits from commercial-training and folk-simplification rumors.

## 16. Player-safe projection

Even if an institution knows a method:
- the player does not automatically know it;
- discovering a manual, mentor, curriculum, research file, or institutional contact may reveal broad guidance;
- hidden percentages remain hidden;
- exact thresholds remain hidden unless a rare explicit knowledge event establishes them.
