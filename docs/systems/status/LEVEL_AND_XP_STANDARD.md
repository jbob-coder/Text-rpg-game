# THE GAME — Level & XP Standard

Status: **ACTIVE TARGET-GAME DESIGN / NUMERIC CALIBRATION OPEN**

Purpose: define what Level means in canon and what must be decided before implementation.

## 1. Locked canon

- Level is real Status data.
- Kills can grant XP/progression.
- Beast kills count.
- PK counts.
- Level 100 is extraordinarily rare.
- Only two humans in known history have reached Level 100 so far.
- Level 100 is tied to the exceptional possibility of changing/replacing the primary awakened ability.

No exact XP formula is currently canon.

## 2. Level is not total character power

Level coexists with:
- attributes;
- skills;
- primary ability rarity;
- ability mastery;
- techniques;
- passives;
- equipment;
- condition/injury;
- class/profession;
- knowledge;
- tactical experience.

A higher-Level character is generally more developed, but Level alone does not resolve combat.

## 3. XP sources

Confirmed:
- beast kill;
- PK.

Open:
- assists;
- party contribution;
- bosses;
- indirect kills;
- summoned/controlled creature kills;
- environmental kills;
- training XP;
- quest XP;
- noncombat XP;
- discovery XP.

Do not implement unconfirmed sources as canon.

## 4. Kill credit requirements

Future rules must define:
- final blow versus meaningful contribution;
- assist threshold;
- party split;
- distance/participation;
- repeated farming;
- ownership of traps/environmental effects;
- controlled allies;
- kill stealing;
- unconscious/captured targets;
- execution versus combat defeat.

## 5. Anti-exploit requirements

The system must address:
- trivial-target farming;
- repeated killing of helpless targets;
- engineered respawn loops;
- party collusion;
- duplicate event credit;
- save/reload duplication;
- killing targets vastly below meaningful challenge.

The setting may impose mechanical, social, legal, moral, or diminishing-return consequences. Exact mechanisms remain open.

## 6. XP curve

Future calibration must determine:
- XP required per level;
- whether curve is linear/exponential/piecewise;
- milestone levels;
- soft/hard plateaus;
- how Level 100 remains historically extreme;
- expected age/experience distribution by population.

A curve is invalid if ordinary safe farming makes Level 100 plausible for large populations.

## 7. Level rewards

Still open:
- automatic stat increase;
- stat points;
- resource growth;
- technique capacity;
- ability scaling;
- passive interaction;
- class access;
- Status privileges.

The Level system must not make learned skills or training irrelevant.

## 8. Death and legality

PK grants progression, but that does not mean murder is socially/legal neutral.

World systems should separately track:
- crime;
- witnesses;
- reputation;
- faction response;
- institutional response;
- bounty/justice;
- war-zone legality;
- self-defense;
- sanctioned combat.

XP acknowledgement by the cosmic system is not moral approval.

## 9. Beast XP

Future design must define whether XP depends on:
- beast strength;
- rarity/tier;
- Level-equivalent danger;
- contribution;
- condition;
- mutated state;
- repeated species farming.

This must integrate with beast ecology rather than turning every creature into an infinite XP dispenser.

## 10. Level-100 historical validation

A future history document must identify the two known Level-100 humans, when they reached it, and what happened afterward.

Until authored, their names and details remain open.

## 11. Reconstruction acceptance

Implementation cannot begin until the design can answer:
- what grants XP;
- how kill credit works;
- how XP scales;
- what Level grants;
- how farming is controlled;
- why Level 100 is almost unheard of;
- how Level interacts with stats/skills/ability mastery.
