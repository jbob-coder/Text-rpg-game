## Audit principle

Training methods for attention, observation, listening, ranging, and memory can be widely known while exact Status qualification remains hidden.

## SEN_0001 — Low-Light Acuity
Current: broadly known.
Assessment: **PLAUSIBLE**.
Public training can be known without exposing exact unlock thresholds.

## SEN_0002 — Motion Focus
Current: public rumored; institutions known.
Assessment: **PLAUSIBLE**.
Public recipes may overstate simple “stare harder” practice; meaningful clutter tracking and confirmation matter.

## SEN_0003 — Peripheral Discipline
Current: public unknown; school rumored; government known; faction secret.
Assessment: **LIKELY OVER-CLASSIFIED**.
Peripheral-awareness training is likely visible in many ordinary disciplines.

## SEN_0004 — Sound Separation
Current: public/school unknown; government/faction classified.
Assessment: **STRONG OVER-CLASSIFICATION FLAG**.
Existence of trained source separation is not inherently secret; only exact thresholds or special doctrine might be.

## SEN_0005 — Scent Memory
Current: public false belief; school partial; government known.
Assessment: **PLAUSIBLE IF FALSE BELIEF IS AUTHORED**.
Candidate false belief:
“Repeated exposure alone makes every odor identifiable.”
Correction:
Recognition requires learned references and confirmation; contamination/novel odors remain uncertain.

## SEN_0006 — Thermal Discrimination
Current: broadly known.
Assessment: **PLAUSIBLE**, especially if ordinary and ability-assisted applications are distinguished.

## SEN_0007 — Vibration Sense
Current: rumored/partial with faction secrecy.
Assessment: **PARTIAL**.
Contact-based vibration training can be public; special applications may be faction-specific.

## SEN_0008 — Range Estimation
Current: unknown everywhere.
Assessment: **STRONG TOO-UNKNOWN FLAG**.
Distance estimation is ordinary enough that total institutional ignorance is implausible without specific world evidence.

## SEN_0009 — Threat Localization
Current: public/school known; government detail classified.
Assessment: **PUBLIC EFFECT PLAUSIBLE / CLASSIFIED DETAIL NEEDS JUSTIFICATION**.
Important distinction: it localizes an already detected cue; it is not supernatural danger sense.

## SEN_0010 — Detail Retention
Current: public false belief; school rumored; government classified; faction secret.
Assessment: **LIKELY OVER-CLASSIFIED**.
Candidate false belief:
“It becomes photographic memory.”
Correction:
It only improves short-term retention of already observed physical details and does not guarantee accuracy.

# Anti-exploit / epistemic rule

Sensory passives must not turn player-facing confidence into hidden objective truth. The game should preserve uncertainty when the character's available signal is uncertain.
