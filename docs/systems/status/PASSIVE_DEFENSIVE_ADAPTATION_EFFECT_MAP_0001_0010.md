## Family guardrails

- These passives improve trained response quality, not raw invulnerability.
- Damage, injury, force, terrain, equipment, range, and conditions remain authoritative.
- No passive reveals hidden threats.
- No passive guarantees a successful block/evasion.
- Same-resolver effects require capped composition.
- Duplicate ownership never stacks.
